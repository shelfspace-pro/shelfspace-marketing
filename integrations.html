FAQ

Straight answers

Can ShelfSpace read our email?

No. Outlook and Gmail connect with send-only permission, so ShelfSpace can send invoices and reminders from your mailbox but can’t open or read anything in it. You can disconnect at any time.

Our IT team blocks outside apps.

That’s common. ShelfSpace gives you a ready-made note for your IT team that says exactly what to approve, and they approve it once for the whole company. Until then your invoices keep going out from our billing address.

We use a personal Gmail account.

Connecting a mailbox needs a company Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 account. Personal @gmail.com addresses can’t connect, and your mail keeps sending from our billing address instead.

Do we have to switch POS or accounting software?

No. Sales come in from your existing POS by export, and books sync to the QuickBooks Online file you already use. Your team keeps working the way it does today.

What happens if the person who connected leaves?

Your invoices automatically go back to sending from our billing address, and your admins get a notice to reconnect. Nothing is lost and nothing is sent twice.
