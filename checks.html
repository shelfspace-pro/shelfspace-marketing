Understanding Your Check

You received a payment
from your retail partner

This check was generated by ShelfSpace on behalf of your retailer. It's real, it's legal, and it's backed by funds in their bank account. Here's everything you need to know.

What is this check?

ShelfSpace generates official checks on behalf of your retail partner. This check is drawn directly from the retailer's bank account — the same way a handwritten check would be.

Your retailer authorized this payment through ShelfSpace — whether it's a weekly consignment settlement based on what sold, or a wholesale payment against an invoice. Either way, every dollar is documented and accounted for.

This is the retailer's real money from their real bank account. ShelfSpace is the technology that creates and delivers the check — we are not a third-party intermediary holding or moving funds.

Legal Framework

Why this is legal

ShelfSpace checks are grounded in well-established federal and state law. The same legal framework that governs every check in the U.S. banking system governs yours.

Federal Law · Since 2004

Check 21 Act

The Check Clearing for the 21st Century Act specifically authorizes "substitute checks" — electronically created check images that carry the same legal enforceability as a handwritten original. ShelfSpace checks are valid substitute checks under this law.

UCC · Article 3

Negotiable Instruments

Article 3 of the Uniform Commercial Code defines the legal requirements for checks as negotiable instruments — an unconditional promise to pay a fixed amount. ShelfSpace checks satisfy every element required by Article 3.

UCC · Article 4

Bank Deposits & Collections

Article 4 governs how banks process check deposits and collections. It establishes the rights and responsibilities of every party in the clearing chain — from deposit to settlement between banks.

Bottom line: A ShelfSpace check has the same legal standing as a check your retailer would write by hand. The difference is that ours is generated by software — making it more accurate, more traceable, and faster to deliver.
An Upgrade, Not a Workaround

Why this is better than
a handwritten check

ShelfSpace checks aren't a substitute for the "real thing." They are the real thing — with several significant improvements built in.

Eliminates human error

Check amounts are auto-calculated from settlement data. No manual math, no transposed digits, no discrepancies.

Tamper-evident chain of custody

Every check has a digital audit trail from the moment it's created. No one can alter the amount, payee, or date after issuance.

Optimized MICR line

Machine-printed MICR encoding is optimized for bank scanners, reducing misreads and deposit rejections compared to handwritten checks.

Tied to settlement data

Every check references a specific settlement report with a complete product-level breakdown of what sold and what you're owed.

Verified and trackable

Check authenticity can be verified by anyone — you, your bank, or your accountant — via the ShelfSpace verification portal.

Instant delivery

No waiting for mail. Checks are delivered by email the moment the settlement is approved — ready to print and deposit immediately.

Depositing Your Check

How to deposit

You have two options. Both work. Choose whichever is most convenient for you.

Recommended

Mobile Deposit

1
Print the check page on standard 8.5" x 11" white paper using any regular printer.
2
Cut along the dashed line to separate the check from the instruction sheet.
3
Endorse the back: sign your name and write "For Mobile Deposit Only" (or check the box on the back).
4
Photograph the front and back using your bank's mobile app, just like any other check.
Also Works

Bank Teller

1
Print the full page on standard 8.5" x 11" white paper. Color or black and white both work.
2
Don't cut. Take the entire printed sheet to your bank — the teller can process it as-is.
3
Deposit it with the teller like any other check. If asked, let them know it's a Check 21 substitute check.

Important notes

Do NOT use an ATM to deposit this check
Standard 8.5" x 11" white paper is all you need
Any regular printer — no special ink or paper required
Color or black and white printing both work fine
