FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report showing every unrecovered vendor credit — waste, returns, and co-marketing. No commitment, no credit card. See a sample evaluation.

When does pricing start?

After the evaluation, once you decide to move forward. Core AP and consignment are billed monthly starting day one of service. Credit recovery is billed on each monthly recovery cycle. The evaluation is the free part.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

ShelfSpace handles AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, vendor calls, credit recovery. Your buyer keeps product selection, vendor negotiation, sell-through analysis, and promotional planning. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. We sync to QuickBooks in real time, so your bookkeeper sees clean entries and closes faster.

Does this actually pay for itself?

Yes — and most operators see it become net-positive within 90 days. Core AP at $1,800–$2,500/month per location is roughly equivalent to the bookkeeper hours you free up. Credit recovery is success-based at 25% — the average evaluation finds $8,000–$25,000/month in unrecovered credits. After our 25% fee, you keep 75% — that's $6,000–$19,000/month in net profit on top of the AP value. AP runs cost-comparable, credit recovery runs profit-positive.

Do I have to use both modules?

No. Core AP and Credit Recovery work standalone. Most operators run both — Core AP is the spine, Credit Recovery is the success-based profit add-on. We make money on Credit Recovery only when you do.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access. We never modify your compliance data. All data is encrypted and isolated per operator.

Can I cancel?

Month to month. No long-term commitments. If we're not delivering value, you'll know from the numbers.
