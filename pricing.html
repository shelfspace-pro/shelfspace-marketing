Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report on your AP, your consignment opportunity, and unrecovered vendor credits — waste, returns, and co-marketing. No commitment, no credit card. See a sample evaluation.

Does the service actually pay for itself?

Most operators run net-positive within 90 days. Core AP at $2,500/mo for your first location and $1,800/mo for each additional is roughly equivalent to the bookkeeper hours you free up. Add Credit Recovery and the average evaluation finds $8,000–$25,000/month in unrecovered vendor credits — after our 25% bundled success fee, you keep $6,000–$19,000/month in net profit on top of the AP value. Or run Credit Recovery standalone at 30% with no monthly fee — pay only when we recover. Core AP runs cost-comparable; Credit Recovery runs profit-positive.

Can I do credit recovery without AP?

Yes. Standalone Credit Recovery is 30% of what we recover, no monthly fee — pay only when we recover. Most operators see first credits within 14 days. Add Core AP later and the recovery rate drops to 25%. No contract.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

ShelfSpace handles AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, every vendor email and call, credit recovery. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. We sync to QuickBooks in real time, so your bookkeeper sees clean entries and closes faster. More for firms →

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. Some operators start with standalone Credit Recovery (30%, no monthly fee) and add Core AP once they trust the results — at which point the recovery rate drops to 25%. Others start with Core AP and add Credit Recovery after seeing the evaluation. Each new location runs at the $1,800/mo additional rate; at five or more you can move to Enterprise for a custom quote. Month-to-month, no long-term commitments.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access. We never modify your compliance data. All data is encrypted and isolated per operator.

Do vendors pay anything?

No. The fee stays on your side. Vendors get the portal, settlement reports, and ShelfiQ at no cost.
