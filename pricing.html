Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report on what your AP is leaking, what your vendors owe you, and what's trapped on your shelves. No commitment, no credit card. See a sample evaluation.

What counts as an artifact?

Three things, $20 each: a wholesale payment (a vendor payment the platform generates for you to sign and send), a consignment payment (a settlement payout the platform runs and you approve), and a credit memo (a Metrc-backed vendor credit the platform builds and sends). Free to start — you only pay when the platform produces one. Multi-location and high-volume operators get volume discounts.

Is there a subscription or monthly fee?

No. Free to start, $20 per artifact, nothing recurring. No software seats, no per-location fee, no percentage of your money. Month-to-month, cancel any time. The free evaluation comes first — we show you the dollars on the table before you pay anything.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

The platform runs AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, credit recovery. ShelfiQ handles first-line vendor email; your AP person handles the judgment calls. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. The platform syncs to QuickBooks in real time, so your bookkeeper sees clean entries and closes faster. More for firms →

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access — we never modify your compliance data. All data encrypted and isolated per operator.
