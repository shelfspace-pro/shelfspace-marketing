The fine print, in plain English

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Your price is set by your monthly sales, never by how many invoices or payments you run. We quote it after your free money review, so you see what you're owed before you see a price. For most dispensaries, the credits we recover cover the fee.

What if the credits don't cover the fee?

Then we refund the difference. If the credits we recover in your first 90 days come to less than you paid us, we pay back the gap. Brands get the same promise on the past-due payments we collect.

Do I lose control of my money?

No. We do the work, and nothing moves until you approve it: every check, every credit, every adjustment. You see everything in your own portal.

What happens to my bookkeeper?

They keep close, tax, and 280E. We take the day-to-day invoice and payment work off their plate and keep QuickBooks current, so their month-end gets faster. More for bookkeepers →

How much work is it to switch?

Very little. We connect to Metrc and QuickBooks, load your vendors and open invoices, and run the setup ourselves. Your team approves; we handle the rest.
