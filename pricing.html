Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report on your AP, your consignment opportunity, and unrecovered vendor credits — waste, returns, and co-marketing. No commitment, no credit card. See a sample evaluation.

Does the service actually pay for itself?

Most operators run net-positive within 90 days. Core AP at $1,800–$2,500/mo per location is roughly equivalent to the bookkeeper hours you free up. Add Credit Recovery and the average evaluation finds $8,000–$25,000/month in unrecovered vendor credits — after our 25% success fee, you keep $6,000–$19,000/month in net profit on top of the AP value. Core AP runs cost-comparable; Credit Recovery runs profit-positive.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

ShelfSpace handles AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, every vendor email and call, credit recovery. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. We sync to QuickBooks in real time, so your bookkeeper sees clean entries and closes faster. More for firms →

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. Most operators start with Core AP and add Credit Recovery once they see the evaluation results. Multi-Location operators upgrade as they add stores. Month-to-month, no long-term commitments.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access. We never modify your compliance data. All data is encrypted and isolated per operator.
