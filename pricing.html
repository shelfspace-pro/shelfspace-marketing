Frequently asked questions

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Pricing is custom. Every operation is different, so we scope your accounts payable, consignment, accounts receivable, and credit recovery to your business — delivered as software you drive or done for you. Seeing what you're owed is always free. Tell us about your operation →

Is ShelfSpace free to start?

Seeing what you're owed is free — the credits your vendors owe you, your overdue invoices, your settlements, all reconciled to Metrc. That free money-review is the first step; from there we scope the work with you.

Do you run it for us, or do we?

Either. Some operators want the software and drive it themselves; others want us to run accounts payable, accounts receivable, consignment, and credit recovery for them. We decide which fits in the conversation, and you always approve anything that moves money.

Will collections hurt my vendor relationships?

No. Reminders go out on a steady, professional cadence in your name, not a collections agency's, and every one ends in a one-click Pay Now link. When a shop is genuinely short, you offer a 0% installment plan instead of an ultimatum. You collect the money and keep the account.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

We're your bookkeeper's best friend. We run accounts payable and receivable — invoice processing, matching, vendor payments, credit recovery, collections — and sync clean entries to QuickBooks in real time. Your bookkeeper keeps month-end close, reconciliations, payroll, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E. Their close gets faster, not bigger. More for firms →

How fast will we see results?

Day 1 we audit your last 90 days of vendor activity. First credits are identified within 14 days, consignment settlements run within 30, and the full AP operation is running within 60.
