The fine print, in plain English

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

One flat monthly price, set by your monthly wholesale sales. No per-invoice or per-payment fees, and we never take a cut of what we collect. We go over your price on the demo.

What if you don't collect?

Then you don't pay. If we don't collect 25% of your past-due invoices in the first month, the month is free.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Your first 30 days are free, with nothing to pay up front, no contract and no setup fee. Book a demo and we set it up with you.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. If we aren't delivering, stop whenever you like. Nothing more is owed.

Will my stores know it's you?

Yes. Every invoice and reminder goes out in your name. Stores pay from the email with a one-click Pay Now link, by e-check or bank transfer (ACH), with no login.

What about my sales reps?

They get back to selling. Add your whole team and every rep at no extra cost, and each rep gets a weekly “who owes what” list for their stores.

Does it work with QuickBooks?

Yes, both ways. Connect QuickBooks and we import every open invoice and customer automatically, so there's nothing to re-enter. When a store pays, the payment lands in QuickBooks on its own. Record a payment in QuickBooks and ShelfSpace marks it paid and stops chasing that store. Your books and your collections always match.

How much work is it to switch?

Very little. Connect QuickBooks and your open invoices and customers come over automatically. We contact every past-due store. You see every invoice, reminder and payment in your own portal.
