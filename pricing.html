Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report showing every unrecovered vendor credit — waste, returns, and co-marketing. No commitment, no credit card.

When does pricing start?

After the evaluation, once you decide to move forward. Credit recovery pricing starts from day one of active recovery. The evaluation is the free part — there's no trial period.

Do I have to use all three modules?

No. Each module works standalone. Most operators start with credit recovery and add consignment or AP when they're ready.

How does bundle pricing work?

Credit recovery rates improve when you add consignment or AP. We manage more of your vendor operation, so we recover more efficiently. All three together gives you the lowest total cost.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access. We never modify your compliance data. All data is encrypted and isolated per operator.

Can I cancel?

Yes. No contracts. No minimums. Cancel any time.
