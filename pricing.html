How we price

Three services. One philosophy.

No flat seat fees. Every dollar you pay us is anchored to dollars we put back in your account.

01
Accounts Payable

Priced on usage.

Your AP fee scales with what we actually do for you — invoices processed, vendors paid, vendor inbox handled. Not a software seat license. Set after the free evaluation, locked month-to-month, no recurring inflation.

What's covered: every invoice verified, every vendor email and call answered, every payment cut, QuickBooks synced live.
02
Consignment

Priced on usage.

Move any vendor to consignment — they own the inventory until it sells, you settle weekly. You pay for what you actually run through: settlements processed, vendors active, dollars moved. No flat fee, no minimums. The cash you free up funds the next category you've been meaning to test.

What's covered: vendor agreements, weekly settlements, sell-through reporting, the math.
03
Credit Recovery

Commission-based. Nothing if we don't recover.

Returns, expirations, co-marketing dollars — money your vendors already owe under existing agreements. We find it, document it, and collect it. You pay a share of what lands in your account, and only what lands. Bundle with Core AP and the rate drops further.

What's covered: every recoverable credit category, vendor approvals handled, Metrc-backed memos.

5+ locations get custom enterprise pricing — volume scaling, a dedicated success manager, custom onboarding, and quarterly founder review. Every fee is set after the free evaluation; no commitment until you see the numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What does the free evaluation include?

We connect to your Metrc account, analyze your historical data, and deliver a detailed report on what your AP is leaking, what your vendors owe you, and what's trapped on your shelves. No commitment, no credit card. See a sample evaluation.

Why don't you publish your prices?

Because no two dispensaries leak money the same way, and a one-size monthly fee either undercharges the smallest stores or overcharges the biggest. We set your fee after the evaluation, when we both know what's actually on the table. The fee is always smaller than what we put back in your account — that's the deal.

Does the service actually pay for itself?

Every engagement is priced to be net-positive against the dollars we find for you. If the evaluation doesn't show clear net-positive math, we don't take you on. Month-to-month, no contracts; if the value stops outpacing the fee, you walk.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

We handle AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, every vendor email and call, credit recovery. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. We sync to QuickBooks in real time, so your bookkeeper sees clean entries and closes faster. More for firms →

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use read-only Metrc access — we never modify your compliance data. All data encrypted and isolated per operator.
