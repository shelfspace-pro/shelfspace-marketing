5A.3 Title Retention and Security Interest (Consignment)

For consignment (scan-based trading) transactions facilitated through the Platform, the following legal framework applies:

  • Title Retention. Vendors retain full legal title to all consignment products delivered to a Retailer until the moment immediately preceding the sale to a consumer at the point of sale. Immediately prior to the point of sale, title transfers from Vendor to Retailer, and Retailer then sells to the consumer as the seller of record.
  • Security Interest. Retailer grants Vendor a limited Purchase Money Security Interest (PMSI) restricted to the specific products delivered by that Vendor and the proceeds thereof. This interest does not extend to Retailer's other assets or inventory supplied by other vendors. Retailer authorizes Vendor to file UCC-1 Financing Statements to perfect this limited interest.
  • Risk of Loss. Retailer assumes the risk of loss for consignment products upon acceptance (whether via state track-and-trace system or physical possession). Retailer is responsible for shrinkage, theft, damage, or loss while products are in Retailer's custody, subject to exceptions for product defects attributable to Vendor, regulatory seizure related to Vendor's compliance failures, and force majeure events.
  • Inventory Retrieval. Vendors may retrieve unsold consignment products by scheduling an appointment during Retailer's normal business hours, complying with Retailer's visitor and security procedures, and completing all required state track-and-trace transfers. Retailer shall cooperate in good faith with retrieval requests within seven (7) calendar days.
  • Regulatory Compliance. Retailer is the seller of record to the consumer for regulatory and tax purposes and is solely responsible for collecting and remitting all applicable cannabis excise taxes, state sales taxes, and local taxes. This seller-of-record designation does not alter Vendor's retention of title or either party's characterization of transactions for income tax purposes. 5A.4 Settlement Terms

Settlements are calculated by the Platform based on commercial terms established within the Platform and point-of-sale data provided by the Retailer. The commercial terms recorded in the Platform at the time of inventory acceptance are the agreed-upon terms for that inventory.

  • Consignment Settlement Calculation. Consignment settlements are calculated as the Vendor's contracted share (the profit split recorded in the partnership) of the actual point-of-sale proceeds for consignment inventory sold during the settlement period, net of return and waste credits, conversion credits, and any applied credit memos. Retail pricing and discounting are within Retailer's sole discretion; because the Vendor is paid a share of the actual sale price, any price change or discount is shared proportionally between the parties through the split, and no separate discount budget, margin deficit, or true-up adjustment is calculated. The foregoing applies unless the Retailer elects invoice-price settlement in the partnership record, in which case the Vendor is paid its invoiced unit price for each unit sold — regardless of the point-of-sale price — and the Retailer keeps any amount above that invoiced price and absorbs any discount below it; where a sold unit has no invoiced unit price on record, that unit is settled on the standard split basis described above.
  • Consumer Returns and Vendor Credits. When a consumer return is recorded in the Retailer's point-of-sale system, the Platform will generate a credit memo against amounts owed to the applicable Vendor. Credits are calculated based on the return price and the vendor split percentage applicable at the time of the original sale. Credits are applied to the settlement for the sales cycle in which the return was processed. Each credit memo includes documentation of the product, quantity, return reason, and date.
  • Settlement Finality. In the event of any discrepancy between Platform records and a party's internal records, the Settlement Report and underlying Platform data shall control absent clear and convincing evidence of material error.
