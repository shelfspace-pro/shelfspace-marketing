Under the scan-based trading model facilitated by ShelfSpace, vendors place products on consignment with retailers. Retailers pay vendors only for products sold to end consumers, as reported through integrated POS systems or state track-and-trace data. Settlements are calculated and processed on a weekly basis.

5.2 Settlement Accuracy

5A.3 Title Retention and Security Interest (Consignment)

For consignment (scan-based trading) transactions facilitated through the Platform, the following legal framework applies:

  • Title Retention. Vendors retain full legal title to all consignment products delivered to a Retailer until the moment immediately preceding the sale to a consumer at the point of sale. Immediately prior to the point of sale, title transfers from Vendor to Retailer, and Retailer then sells to the consumer as the seller of record.
  • Security Interest. Retailer grants Vendor a limited Purchase Money Security Interest (PMSI) restricted to the specific products delivered by that Vendor and the proceeds thereof. This interest does not extend to Retailer's other assets or inventory supplied by other vendors. Retailer authorizes Vendor to file UCC-1 Financing Statements to perfect this limited interest.
  • Risk of Loss. Retailer assumes the risk of loss for consignment products upon acceptance (whether via state track-and-trace system or physical possession). Retailer is responsible for shrinkage, theft, damage, or loss while products are in Retailer's custody, subject to exceptions for product defects attributable to Vendor, regulatory seizure related to Vendor's compliance failures, and force majeure events.
  • Inventory Retrieval. Vendors may retrieve unsold consignment products by scheduling an appointment during Retailer's normal business hours, complying with Retailer's visitor and security procedures, and completing all required state track-and-trace transfers. Retailer shall cooperate in good faith with retrieval requests within seven (7) calendar days.
  • Regulatory Compliance. Retailer is the seller of record to the consumer for regulatory and tax purposes and is solely responsible for collecting and remitting all applicable cannabis excise taxes, state sales taxes, and local taxes. This seller-of-record designation does not alter Vendor's retention of title or either party's characterization of transactions for income tax purposes. Each party shall consult its own tax advisor regarding proper tax treatment.

5A.4 Settlement Terms

Settlements are calculated by the Platform based on commercial terms established within the Platform and point-of-sale data provided by the Retailer. The commercial terms recorded in the Platform at the time of inventory acceptance are the agreed-upon terms for that inventory.

  • Discount Framework. The Platform calculates a total discount budget for each settlement period based on (a) authorized aging markdowns per the aging schedule in the partnership record, and (b) the operational discount allowance agreed upon in the partnership record (or a default rate if none is specified). If actual discounts given by Retailer at point of sale exceed the total discount budget, a margin deficit (true-up) will be calculated and reflected in the settlement.
  • Consumer Returns and Vendor Credits. When a consumer return is recorded in the Retailer's point-of-sale system, the Platform will generate a credit memo against amounts owed to the applicable Vendor. Credits are calculated based on the return price and the vendor split percentage applicable at the time of the original sale. Credits are applied to the settlement for the sales cycle in which the return was processed. Each credit memo includes documentation of the product, quantity, return reason, and date.
  • Settlement Finality. In the event of any discrepancy between Platform records and a party's internal records, the Settlement Report and underlying Platform data shall control absent clear and convincing evidence of material error.

5A.5 Multi-Location Retailers

Each licensed retail premises operates as a separate unit within the Platform with distinct commercial terms, inventory records, and settlements per location. A default or dispute at one location does not automatically constitute a default at another location, except in cases of fraud, material misrepresentation, or repeated defaults across multiple locations. Retailers shall not transfer consignment inventory between locations without the applicable Vendor's prior written consent and compliance with all state track-and-trace requirements.

7. Credit Memos and Credit Recovery

7.1 Manual Credit Memos

Retailers and vendors may create credit memos for product returns, co-marketing discounts, or other agreed-upon adjustments. Credit memos follow a defined workflow: proposed, accepted or rejected, applied against future payments, and ultimately settled. Both parties are responsible for reviewing and responding to credit memos in a timely manner.

7.2 Automated Credit Memos

When enabled by a retailer, ShelfSpace may automatically generate credit memos based on sales data, inventory cost data, and partnership terms. Automated credit memos include two types: (a) returns credits for product waste, damage, or destruction; and (b) co-marketing credits for products sold below expected retail margin (below-keystone pricing recovery). The primary data source for automated credit memo generation is the state track-and-trace system (e.g., Metrc); supplemental data sources (POS exports, invoice-derived costs) may be used on a case-by-case basis. Automated credit memos are subject to the same review and approval workflow as manual credit memos. Retailers are responsible for reviewing auto-generated credit memos for accuracy before approval.

7.3 Credit Recovery Services

ShelfSpace identifies, documents, and facilitates the recovery of vendor credits owed to retailers. Credit recovery services include analysis of sales data against wholesale costs to identify margin recovery opportunities, generation of credit memos on behalf of retailers, and communication with vendors for review and approval. ShelfSpace charges a commission fee calculated as a percentage of approved credit memo amounts, as described in Section 12. The commission fee is earned when a credit memo reaches "Approved" or "Accepted" status. ShelfSpace does not guarantee any specific recovery amount. Retailers are responsible for reviewing identified credits for accuracy.

7.4 Vendor Review and Response

Vendors receive credit memo proposals — whether delivered individually or aggregated within a Monthly Credit Statement — via email and through the ShelfSpace Platform. Vendors may respond by approving the proposal, counter-proposing a different amount, or opening a discussion regarding any line item. Credit memo and Monthly Credit Statement proposals are not approved by silence, and vendor inaction does not constitute acceptance. A proposal remains pending vendor review until the vendor takes action or the matter is otherwise resolved under these Terms.

ShelfSpace will follow up with vendors at regular intervals via email reminders, in-Platform notifications, and additional communications generated through the Platform until the vendor responds. Reminder cadence and channels are at ShelfSpace's reasonable discretion and may be adjusted from time to time without notice.

Stale Statement Escalation. If a credit memo or Monthly Credit Statement has not been approved or voided within ninety (90) calendar days of its date of delivery, ShelfSpace will notify the affected retailer's designated administrators by email. Following such notice, the retailer's administrators may, in their discretion, elect to (a) approve the credit memo or statement at the originally proposed amount, which shall constitute final acceptance for purposes of these Terms, (b) void the credit memo or statement, or (c) continue to await vendor response. The stale-statement notification is itself informational and does not, of itself, constitute approval, rejection, or modification of the proposal.

Reserved Optional Behaviors. ShelfSpace reserves the right, with at least thirty (30) days' written notice provided via email or the Platform, to enable additional response-driven behaviors for credit memos and Monthly Credit Statements, including (a) an automatic approval mechanism after a defined vendor response window, (b) a hold on outgoing payment instrument generation to a vendor while a credit memo or Monthly Credit Statement involving that vendor has not been approved or voided, or (c) other procedural behaviors consistent with these Terms. Such behaviors will not take effect until the notice period elapses.

7.5 Credit Memo and Monthly Credit Statement Lifecycle

Credit memos and Monthly Credit Statements remain in effect until they are approved, voided, or otherwise resolved under these Terms. ShelfSpace and authorized retailer administrators may void credit memos or Monthly Credit Statements at any time, including following a vendor's request to open discussion or an administrator's election under Section 7.4. ShelfSpace may send reminder notifications prior to voiding but is not obligated to do so. Voided credit memos and Monthly Credit Statements have no effect on settlement obligations and cannot be reactivated.

7.6 Monthly Credit Statements

ShelfSpace may generate monthly credit statements aggregating approved credit memos per vendor-retailer pair. Monthly credit statements serve as consolidated records for credit application. Statement amounts are binding upon delivery unless disputed within the dispute window described in Section 6.5.
