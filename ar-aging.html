FAQ

Straight answers

What does AR Aging show me?

Every open invoice, grouped by how late it is: current, then 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–28 and 29+ days late. Switch to monthly for 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+. Click any bucket to see the stores in it.

Can I see one rep's accounts?

Yes. Filter by rep, by customer, or search by payer, invoice or PO number. The buckets and totals update to match.

What happens when I launch reminders?

Each account you picked gets an email with a Pay Now link and the invoice attached. Reminders follow on the schedule you choose while anything is past due, worded for how late the balance is. Nothing is drawn until each store authorizes payment.

Is my sales rep copied?

If you want. Tick "CC sales rep" and the rep is copied on the email and every reminder. Replies go to your AR assistant, which copies the rep.

Do transfers to my own stores count as receivables?

No. Transfers to stores you own are shown separately and kept out of the total, so the number at the top is what other stores actually owe you.
