FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you just run collections for me?

Yes — that's the done-for-you option. You connect once, and we run the whole collecting cycle in your name: the standing Pay Now links, the escalating follow-up, the partial-payment and installment offers. The retailer pays straight into your account, and you keep the relationship. We scope it to your operation — talk to us.

What is a standing pay link?

A standing pay link is a single Pay Now link that lives on every invoice you send a retailer. It never expires and needs no login — the retailer clicks it, enters their bank and routing number, signs on their phone, and a real digital check is generated on their account payable to you. Because the link is always there, every dunning email you send already has a one-click way to pay built in.

How does escalating dunning work?

Once an invoice is due, ShelfSpace runs the follow-up for you on a schedule that escalates in tone as the invoice ages — a friendly heads-up at the due date, a firmer reminder a week later, and so on. Every message carries the standing pay link, and the reminders stop on their own the moment the retailer pays. You're never the one typing "just following up" again.

Can a retailer pay part of an invoice or pay over time?

Yes. Collections supports partial payments and installment plans, so a slow-paying retailer can clear a balance in pieces instead of ghosting the whole invoice. There's no interest and no processor — each payment is a real digital check drawn on the retailer's bank, and the balance and schedule stay tracked in one place.

What is ACH collect (coming soon)?

Coming soon: ACH collect will pull an authorized payment straight from the retailer's bank to yours, bank-to-bank, with no processor in the middle to freeze or shut off. It's part of the collecting we run; a mailed paper check is there when you want one.

Is this legal and safe in cannabis?

Yes. Every payment is a real digital check or an authorized ACH transfer drawn on the retailer's own bank account, payable to you. ShelfSpace never holds or moves the money, which is exactly why it works where card processors and payment apps get shut off.
