The Engine

Weekly settlements, calculated to the penny.

Every week, the platform pulls your sales data, reconciles inventory, applies your per-category profit splits to what each item actually rang up for, and generates a five-page settlement report for each vendor.

  • Weekly settlement runs from POS/Metrc data
  • Five-page PDF settlement reports
  • Category-level profit splits, set per vendor
  • Returns and shrinkage reconciliation
  • Every line rounded to the penny before it totals
  • Full audit trail on every calculation

Payments

Check 21 compliant. Every vendor. Every week.

The platform generates federally compliant checks for every vendor payment. Vendors download and deposit instantly — mobile banking or print at home. Prefer to skip the check? Pay a vendor by ACH bank transfer instead — opt-in, straight to their bank.

  • Check 21 & UCC Article 3 compliant
  • Print on standard paper, mobile deposit ready
  • ACH bank transfer — opt-in direct deposit, funds to the vendor's bank
  • Void a check and re-pay for a fresh replacement
  • Real-time QuickBooks sync
  • Vendor notification on issuance
  • Complete payment audit trail
