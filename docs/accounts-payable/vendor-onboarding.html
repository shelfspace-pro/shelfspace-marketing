At a Glance

  • No paperwork, no W-9 chasing, no back-and-forth

How Cannabis Vendor Onboarding Works

Getting a new vendor into your payment workflow should not take days of emails and paperwork. When you need to onboard a cannabis vendor on ShelfSpace, you add the vendor and the platform sends a single email invite. The vendor clicks the link, creates their portal account, and enters their banking and contact information. That is the entire process.

From your side, you provide the vendor name and email address. The platform handles the rest -- the invite, the follow-up if they have not responded, and the portal walkthrough if they need help. Most vendors complete setup in under ten minutes. Learn more about cannabis accounts payable and how onboarding fits into the broader workflow.

What the platform runs: Sending vendor invites, following up on incomplete signups, verifying portal accounts, mapping vendors to your QuickBooks file, and ShelfiQ answering vendor questions during setup.

What Vendors See

When a vendor receives your invite, they land on a branded setup page. They create a login, enter their business details, and provide banking information for check deposits. The entire experience is self-service and guided. No phone calls. No faxed forms.

Once onboarded, vendors get access to the vendor portal where they can view payments, download checks, review invoices, and respond to credit memos. They see only what relates to your dispensary -- nothing from other retailers on the platform.

Cannabis and Non-Cannabis Vendors

The platform handles both cannabis and non-cannabis vendors through the same onboarding flow. Whether you are bringing on a flower distributor, an edibles brand, or your packaging supplier, the process is identical. Every vendor gets a portal, every vendor receives payments the same way, and every vendor shows up in your QuickBooks file with proper categorization.

Bulk Onboarding

If you are moving your entire vendor list to ShelfSpace, the platform supports bulk onboarding. Provide a CSV or spreadsheet with vendor names and email addresses, and the platform sends invites to the full list at once. It tracks who has completed setup and sends follow-up reminders to stragglers so you do not have to.

For dispensaries with 20 or more vendors, this saves hours of individual outreach. Most vendors activate within one to two weeks of the initial bulk invite.

After Onboarding

Once a vendor is active, they are immediately available for payments. Their profile syncs to QuickBooks, their banking details are stored securely, and they appear in your AP dashboard. The next time you have an invoice from that vendor, the platform can verify it and generate a payment without any additional setup.
