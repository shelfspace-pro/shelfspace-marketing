Why ShelfSpace Cannabis Dispensaries Choose Us

ShelfSpace cannabis is a managed operations platform built for dispensaries that work with dozens — sometimes hundreds — of vendors. We handle consignment, accounts payable, and credit recovery under one roof, so you get a single engine for every vendor relationship and every payment.

Most dispensaries cobble together spreadsheets, QuickBooks workarounds, and manual check runs. That works until it doesn't. ShelfSpace replaces the patchwork with a purpose-built system that we operate for you.

What we handle: Vendor onboarding, settlement calculations, check payments, credit memo workflows, QuickBooks sync, and reporting. You review and approve — we do the rest.

Three Services, One Platform

Consignment. Vendors place inventory on your shelves and get paid after it sells. We calculate weekly settlements based on your POS data, apply category-level profit splits and aging discounts, then cut the check. Learn how consignment works.

Accounts Payable. For wholesale purchases, we verify invoices against delivery records, generate Check 21 payments, and sync everything to QuickBooks. No more manual data entry or lost invoices. See the AP overview.

Credit Recovery. Returns, expiring product, and co-marketing credits often fall through the cracks. We track every credit memo from creation through vendor approval and final deduction, so you recover what you're owed. Explore credit recovery.

The Managed Service Model

ShelfSpace is not self-serve software you have to learn and operate. We assign your account a dedicated team that configures your vendor terms, processes your weekly settlements, and handles vendor questions through the vendor portal.

Your role is minimal: review settlement summaries, approve payments when you're ready, and reach out if anything looks off. We handle the volume.

How Everything Connects

ShelfSpace pulls sales data from your POS every day, matches it against vendor contracts and inventory records, and produces settlements and payment batches on a weekly cycle. QuickBooks stays in sync. Vendors access their own portal to view payments, download checks, and respond to credit memos.

ShelfiQ, our built-in AI assistant, gives both you and your vendors instant answers — payment status, settlement details, inventory lookups — without waiting on email.
