What Gets Flagged

When it doesn't match, you know why.

A flag always comes with the reason, so the fix is obvious.

Quantity

Short or over

The invoice bills more (or less) than the manifest carried.

Product

Not on the manifest

A line was billed that never shipped in Metrc.

Timing

No shipment yet

The invoice went out before the product did. It re-checks when the manifest lands.

Duplicate

Billed twice

Two invoices point at the same delivery.

Split

One invoice, several deliveries

Matched across every manifest it covers, not flagged.

Size

Right strain, wrong size

An eighth billed, a gram shipped. Caught at the line, not the total.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ShelfVerify?

ShelfVerify checks every cannabis invoice line by line against the Metrc manifest that moved the product. Each line is either marked ShelfVerified or flagged with the reason it doesn't match.

Is ShelfVerify a compliance certification?

No. Metrc stays your compliance record. ShelfVerify compares your invoices to that record so what you pay, or bill, agrees with what actually shipped.

What if one invoice covers several deliveries?

ShelfVerify matches it across every manifest it covers. A split delivery isn't flagged as a problem.

What happens when a line doesn't match?

It's flagged with the reason: short, over, not on the manifest, wrong size, no shipment yet, or billed twice. Retailers can short-pay the difference; vendors see exactly which invoice to fix or re-send.

Do I have to do anything to use it?

Connect your Metrc account once. After that, every invoice is matched automatically, and we work the exceptions with you.

Is ShelfVerify included?

Yes. ShelfVerify runs inside ShelfPay for retailers and ShelfCollect for vendors. See our pricing page, or talk to us.
