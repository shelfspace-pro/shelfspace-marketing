FAQ

Straight answers

Can wholesale cannabis buyers pay invoices over time?

Yes. You offer a 0% payment plan on an open invoice: weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, from 2 to 24 payments. The dispensary picks Pay over time on its payment link, sets it up, paying from its bank account by ACH bank transfer or e-check, and you approve it. More on getting paid on net terms in our payment plans guide.

Is there interest on a payment plan?

No. Every plan is 0% interest with no finance charge. The store pays the invoice balance, split into equal payments.

What happens if a payment bounces?

Report it in one click from the invoice and pick the bank’s reason. The payment is reversed in the app and in QuickBooks, and the plan pauses. For an ordinary bounce you can resume: the next payment is at least 3 days out and the bounced amount becomes one payment at the end. Or cancel, and what’s left becomes a normal invoice with reminders.

How does the store pay each installment?

From the store’s own bank account, by ACH bank transfer or e-check, on each scheduled date. A store that saved its bank for that location sets the plan up in one click. It gets a heads-up 3 days before each payment and a receipt after it. See every way stores can pay on Payments.

Who approves the payment plan?

You do, from the invoice page. Nothing is taken until you approve, and the first payment goes out at approval. You get a reminder after 2 days, and a request left unanswered for 7 days is declined automatically and the invoice goes back to normal reminders.

Can the store stop the payment plan?

Yes. Once a plan is set up, the store’s payment link becomes a plan-status page with the schedule, what’s paid so far and the next date, plus a Stop future payments button. Payments already made stay paid.
