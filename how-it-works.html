How It Works

Three services. One team. Here's how each one works.

Consignment, credit recovery, and accounts payable — each one runs differently, but they're all managed by us.

Not Another Portal to Manage

We do the work. You run the dispensary.

We're not software you have to learn — we're a team that handles your vendor finances. We run your consignment settlements, recover your vendor credits, manage your AP, and cut your checks. When your vendors have questions about payments, invoices, or their account — they email us, not you. Our goal is that you never need to log in. We just do the tedious AP work, save you money, and send you the reports.

Consignment
$250,000 in freed capital

Stop paying upfront for inventory. We run the weekly settlements — you free up cash and carry zero risk.

Full consignment details
01
We Handle This

We set up the contracts

We draft a consignment agreement between you and each vendor — covering profit splits, shrinkage policies, discounts, and payment terms. You review and sign. We handle the legal framework.

02
We Handle This

Your vendors deliver, we track everything

Inventory flows through your supply chain like normal. We tie into your POS and METRC to track every sale, every return, and every unit on your shelves — and calculate the weekly settlement.

Five-page settlement report: Vendor payout table, returns detail, discount audit, remaining inventory with velocity metrics, and a depositable check.
03
We Handle This

We cut the checks

Every week, we generate a Check 21-compliant check for each vendor based on confirmed sales. Vendors download and deposit from their portal. You do nothing.

Credit Recovery
$50,000+/year recovered

Returns, expirations, and co-marketing spend — we find the money your vendors owe you and get it back.

Full credit recovery details
01
We Handle This

We pull your data

We tap into your POS and METRC directly — return records, expiration dates, promo spend. No exports, no spreadsheets. We grab everything we need without interrupting your day.

02
We Handle This

We create credit memos

Every return, expiration, and co-marketing promotion becomes a credit memo — itemized, vendor by vendor, with full documentation. We catch the money most operators never realize they're owed.

03
We Handle This

We get approval and apply credits

We send each credit memo to the vendor, handle the back-and-forth, and apply approved credits to your next settlement or offset against open invoices. Less cash out the door.

Accounts Payable
