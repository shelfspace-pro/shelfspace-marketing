What a late invoice costs you

A late invoice is a loan you never agreed to make. Put in the amount, the due date and your own numbers, and see what it has cost you so far.

How it works </>Embed it on your site

The two costs it counts

Carrying cost. Money a store owes you is money you can't use. If you borrow to cover the gap, it costs you interest. If you don't borrow, it is cash you could have put into inventory or payroll. The calculator multiplies the invoice amount by your yearly cost of money and by the share of a year the invoice has been late.

Time spent chasing. Calls, texts, emails and visits to ask about one invoice add up. Enter the hours and what that person's time costs you.

What it leaves out on purpose

Old invoices get harder to collect, and some never get paid. We don't add a write-off percentage, because we haven't found a published, sourced figure for cannabis wholesale that we trust. If you know your own write-off rate, the carrying cost here is the floor, not the ceiling.

Use your own numbers

  • Cost of money: the yearly rate on the credit line or loan you use to cover the gap. No loan? Use what that cash would earn you elsewhere.
  • Dates: enter them as MM/DD/YYYY. The as-of date starts at today.
  • Hours: count everyone who touched it: you, your sales rep, your bookkeeper.

Lower the cost next time

The cheapest late invoice is one that gets paid on time: invoice on shipment, remind on a schedule and give stores a one-click way to pay. When a store is already behind, a signed promise to pay letter or a payment plan agreement gets you a date instead of another week of silence. Frequently asked questions

How is the carrying cost calculated?

The invoice amount times your yearly cost of money, times the number of days late divided by 365. It is simple interest on the money you are owed. The math is done in whole cents, so the result doesn't drift.

Why doesn't it include bad debt?

Because we would have to invent the number. We have not found a sourced write-off rate for cannabis wholesale invoices by age. If you track your own, add it on top of what the calculator shows.

What cost of money should I use?

Use the yearly rate on the credit line or loan you lean on when stores pay late. If you don't borrow, use what that cash would earn or save you elsewhere, such as an inventory buy you had to delay.

Is anything I type saved?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and sends nothing to ShelfSpace.
