The problem

The big expense tools weren’t built for cannabis.

Capturing a receipt is easy anywhere. Paying your people back from a cannabis company’s bank account is where the mainstream tools stop.

Card programs say no

Brex’s prohibited activities list names marijuana-related activity, including dispensing and deriving revenue from it.

Reimbursements get fenced off

Ramp lists cannabis-related businesses among the industries that can’t use its international reimbursements. Expensify’s help center lists marijuana-related businesses among those it can’t process direct payments for.

Xero’s old expenses are going away

Xero is retiring Classic Expenses and its expense-claims connection for other apps. Spreadsheets and envelopes of receipts fill the gap.

How it works

From a crumpled receipt to money back in four steps.

01

Snap or forward

Open the camera from a home-screen icon, or forward the receipt email to your company’s receipts address. Mileage is typed in: from, to, miles.

02

It’s read and coded

AI reads the merchant, date, total and tax and suggests the account. The employee confirms, picks the company it belongs to, and submits.

03

It’s approved

The manager, a second approver above an amount you set, then finance. Policy flags show up next to the line so nobody has to hunt.

04

Posted and paid

The report lands in Xero or QuickBooks as a bill to the employee, and they’re paid back by bank transfer, digital check, or through payroll.

What it handles

The parts that make cannabis expenses messy.

Several companies, one login

Licenses usually mean separate companies. Each expense names the one it belongs to, and a report splits by company so nothing posts to the wrong books.

Mileage at the right rate

The IRS rate was 72.5¢ a mile through 06/30/2026 and 76¢ from 07/01/2026. Each trip gets the rate for its own date. Try the calculator.

Policy flags, not walls

Set a limit per line, the amount that needs a receipt, and how old an expense can be. Breaks get flagged for the approver instead of blocking the employee.

Duplicate receipts caught

The same receipt submitted twice, by two people, or under two companies gets flagged before anyone pays it.

Everyone sees only their part

Employees see their own expenses. Approvers sign in with a second factor. Turn expenses on by company and by person, so your AR team never sees them.

Paid back three ways

Bank transfer or a digital check from your account, or mark it paid through payroll and hand your payroll provider a CSV.

Xero and QuickBooks

Lands in your books the way your bookkeeper would enter it.

An approved report becomes one bill to the employee in that company’s Xero or QuickBooks Online, each line on its own account (with up to two tracking categories in Xero), the receipts merged into one attached PDF. When the employee is paid, the payment goes on the bill, so the bank line matches.

Several companies on different books? Each report posts to the right one.

Keep reimbursements tax-free

The IRS rules, checked on every line.

Reimbursements stay off an employee’s W-2 when they’re paid under an accountable plan. The platform flags the lines that would break one.

Submitted within 60 days

The IRS treats an expense accounted for within 60 days as on time. Older ones get flagged.

Receipts where they count

Lodging needs a receipt, and so does anything else of $75 or more. A missing one is flagged before approval.

FAQ

Expense reports, answered.

Does it work for plant-touching cannabis businesses?

Yes. ShelfSpace is built for cannabis companies: dispensaries, cultivators, processors and brands. Employees are paid back from your own account by bank transfer or digital check.

Do employees need to download an app?

No. It runs in the phone’s browser and can be saved to the home screen like an app. Employees can also forward receipt emails to your company’s receipts address, which creates a draft for them to finish.

How does it post to Xero or QuickBooks?

Each approved report becomes one bill to the employee in the right company’s Xero or QuickBooks Online, with each line on its account (plus tracking categories in Xero), and every receipt merged into one attached PDF. When the employee is paid, the payment is recorded on the bill.

Can one person work across several of our companies?

Yes. Each expense names the company it belongs to, and a report with lines for two companies is split into two reports, one for each company’s books.

How is mileage paid?

The employee types where they went and how many miles. Each trip is paid at the IRS standard rate for its date: 72.5 cents a mile before 07/01/2026 and 76 cents from 07/01/2026.

Who can approve expenses?

You set it: the employee’s manager, a second approver above an amount you choose, then finance, with a backup for each approver. Nobody can approve their own expense, and approvers sign in with a second factor.

What about company cards?

Today ShelfSpace handles out-of-pocket spending and mileage, the money you owe your people. Ask us on the call about company card spending.
