ShelfiQ Handles the Vendor's Reply

Vendors reply by email — your AI assistant does the rest

Full transcript

Your vendors are busy, and the last thing they want is one more login just to deal with a credit memo. So with ShelfSpace, they don't need one. Here's how a simple email reply turns into a done deal.

Every credit memo you send goes out as an email your vendor can just reply to — no login, no portal, nothing to learn. They write back in plain English, and ShelfiQ, your AI assistant, reads that reply, understands what they decided, and books it for you — approve the whole credit, approve part of it, or push back — automatically.

And it all lands right here, on the memo itself. Every message between you and your vendor, kept together in one thread — so nothing important is buried in an inbox only one person can see.

Here's a real one. Your vendor read the memo and wrote back — the waste credit looks right, but that co-marketing line seems a little high, can we take a look? A fair question, in their own words.

And the answer comes right back in the same thread, tied to the actual numbers behind that line — exactly where the co-marketing charge came from, promo by promo. No phone tag, no digging through spreadsheets. The memo simply waits, and only moves forward once your vendor's satisfied.

So what does your team actually have to do here? Most days, nothing at all — ShelfiQ handles the routine back-and-forth on its own. But you're never locked out. When you want to weigh in, your reply goes right here, and if a vendor ever asks for a real person, or the conversation gets thorny, it's handed straight to you.

That's the whole idea. Your vendors reply the way they'd reply to anyone, ShelfiQ turns those plain words into real decisions on the memo, and your team steps in only when it genuinely wants to. Less chasing, no extra logins, and a credit conversation that mostly runs itself.
