For developers

Connect in two minutes.

Standards all the way down. If your client speaks MCP or OAuth, it already speaks ShelfSpace.

MCP server
https://ourshelf.space/api/mcp
Transport
Streamable HTTP (POST), JSON-RPC 2.0
Auth
OAuth 2.1, authorization code + PKCE (S256), refresh tokens
Discovery
/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server (RFC 8414), /.well-known/oauth-protected-resource (RFC 9728)
Clients
Dynamic registration (RFC 7591), client ID metadata documents, or a confidential app registered in Settings → AI agents
Scopes
shelfspace.read · shelfspace.act
REST
POST /api/agent/v1/tools/{tool} · GET /api/agent/v1/openapi.json
Guide
ourshelf.space/developers/agents
# Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector
Name        ShelfSpace
Server URL  https://ourshelf.space/api/mcp
Auth        OAuth  # you'll sign in and pick stores

# then just ask:
"Which of my stores can you see?"
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "shelfspace": {
      "type": "http",
      "url": "https://ourshelf.space/api/mcp"
    }
  }
}
// OAuth + PKCE runs on first connect
curl -X POST \
  https://ourshelf.space/api/agent/v1/tools/list_my_stores \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for cannabis brands and vendors?

Yes. Brands on ShelfSpace connect the same way. Your AI can see who owes you and how late, send invoices, statements and reminders, and track promises to pay. No Metrc connection required.

Can my AI chase past-due invoices for me?

Yes. Ask who's past due and tell it to send reminders. They go from your billing address with a pay link, and your AI tracks every promise to pay so you know the moment one slips.

Is there an MCP server for cannabis accounting and payments?

Yes. ShelfSpace is a remote MCP server for cannabis back-office finance. Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Meta Muse or any MCP-compatible AI and it can work your accounts payable, receivables, Metrc invoice matching, QuickBooks, credit recovery, consignment settlements and vendor payments, scoped to your own stores.

Can I use ChatGPT or Claude to run my dispensary's accounts payable?

Yes. Add ShelfSpace as a connector in ChatGPT or Claude, sign in once, and pick your stores. Your AI can then find invoices, check them against Metrc, see what's owed, line up vendor payments, upload new invoices and answer questions about any vendor.

Does ShelfSpace work with Meta Muse?

Yes. Register an app in ShelfSpace Settings under AI agents, choose the Meta Muse preset, and paste the Client ID and Client Secret into a Muse custom connector.

Do I need to log in to ShelfSpace to use it from my AI?

Only once. You sign in with your normal login and verification code to connect your AI. After that you work entirely from your AI's chat window.

Can my AI send vendor payments?

Yes, once your company turns on AI payments. You choose whether a person confirms each payment or whether your AI pays within per-payment and per-day limits you set. Bank-detail changes always need a person on your team to confirm.

Can my AI see how my invoices match my Metrc manifests?

Yes. Your AI can pull every invoice with its Metrc match, line by line, and explain why a line is short, over, missing from the manifest, or billed twice.

Can my AI manage QuickBooks through ShelfSpace?

Your AI can see what has synced to QuickBooks, what's waiting and why, so you can catch a stuck bill or payment without opening either system.

Is it safe to connect an AI to my company's finances?

Your AI signs in as you and only sees the stores you choose. A read-only person gets a read-only AI. It never holds a database password, every action is logged as done by your AI, and you can disconnect it in one click.

What is MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard AI assistants use to connect to outside tools and data. ShelfSpace speaks it, so your AI can use ShelfSpace the way a person on your team would.

Is there a REST API too?

Yes. The same tools are available as a REST API at /api/agent/v1/tools/{tool}, described by an OpenAPI 3.1 file, using the same OAuth token, permissions and audit trail as the MCP server.
