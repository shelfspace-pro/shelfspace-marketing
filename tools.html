Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. The calculators and templates are free to use, with no sign-up. The calculators run in your browser and send nothing to ShelfSpace.

Can I put the calculators on my website?

Yes. Each calculator page has embed code you can paste into your site. Please keep the credit line under it.

Are the templates legal advice?

No. They are general business forms. Not legal advice — have your counsel review it before you use it.
