A note from ChrisFounder & CEO, ShelfSpace

I've built brands, sold them into stores, and waited on the check. ShelfSpace is what I wish I'd had.

I started my first cannabis company in Oregon in 2015: vertically integrated, multi-state, the whole thing. I've been a cultivator, a manufacturer, and a retailer. Most of those years I was on the brand side, growing and making product and selling it wholesale into dispensaries. I'm also the Founder & CEO of Diem Cannabis, the dispensary business I still run in Massachusetts today. We exited Oregon in 2023, and that decision changed how I see this whole industry.

Here's what the brand side taught me: making the product is the easy part. Getting paid for it is the hard part. You get on the shelf, the product sells, and the invoice sits in a store's AP pile for weeks. Meanwhile you're the one making payroll, buying inputs and covering the next run. I've made payroll by the skin of my teeth more times than I'd like to admit, with real money sitting on other people's books.

And chasing it is miserable. You don't want to be the brand that nags, because the buyer decides whether you keep your spot. Your reps are supposed to be selling, not collecting. So the follow-up slips, invoices age, and a few slow payers quietly decide your cash flow for you.

Running Diem showed me the other side of that same invoice. Stores usually aren't trying to stiff anyone. They're juggling dozens of vendors across spreadsheets and a buried inbox. The invoice that gets paid is the one that's easy to find, easy to check, and easy to pay. The one that gets lost is the PDF buried in somebody's email.

That's what I built ShelfSpace to fix. We run your accounts receivable for you, in your name. Every receivable in one place, aging tracked on a schedule, and steady, polite follow-up handled for you, with a Pay Now link on every invoice so stores settle in one click. When a store promises to pay, we track whether it really did. Your reps get a weekly summary of what their stores owe. You stop being the one chasing, and the relationship stays clean.

And it works from both sides of the invoice. For the dispensaries we work with, the same team runs accounts payable: every invoice checked against Metrc and vendors paid right and on time. When both sides run clean, none of it is a fight. It's just how good partners work.

Outside of ShelfSpace, my family is everything to me. Being a dad is the most important role in my life, and it's why I work so hard to build something meaningful. I want to help create an industry where good operators can make a real living and build something their families can be proud of. Every brand and store we work with is someone's livelihood, someone's family. That's never lost on me.

Let's fix this industry together. I'm in it with you.

— Chris
