About ShelfSpace

Born in the trenches

Founded by a 10+ year cannabis industry veteran who's been on both sides of the counter.

Chris Mitchem

Founder & CEO

A decade in the trenches. Every lesson the hard way. ShelfSpace is what I wish existed when I needed it most.

I started my first cannabis company in Oregon in 2015 — vertically integrated, multi-state, the whole thing. I've been a cultivator, a manufacturer, and a retailer. I've made payroll by the skin of my teeth, fought through market crashes, and navigated every compliance curveball this industry throws at you. We still operate in Massachusetts today, but we exited Oregon in 2023 — a decision that changed my perspective forever.

Through all of it, I kept seeing the same problems: retailers drowning in AP busywork, vendors waiting months to get paid, and both sides leaving money on the table through missed credits and broken processes. Nobody was solving the financial plumbing between retailers and vendors — the unsexy but critical stuff that determines whether an operator survives or goes under.

I built ShelfSpace to fix what I saw missing. We're an AP team first — we run wholesale invoices, three-way matching, vendor payments on whatever terms each vendor needs, the whole financial relationship between retailer and vendor. That includes the inbox: every vendor email about payments, balances, and credits comes to us, not you — most answered in under a minute with live Metrc data. That's the spine. Then consignment fits in naturally — it's just one of the payment terms we run, and it unlocks the cash trapped in retailer shelves. Credit recovery is the third leg — every month we recover the credits your vendor agreements already promised: returns, expirations, co-marketing, the money most retailers leave on the table because the work is too tedious to chase.

Today, ShelfSpace handles it all: AP, vendor payments on any terms, consignment settlements, credit recovery. Your buyer keeps the product and vendor work. Your bookkeeper keeps the close and reporting. We handle the AP underneath. One platform, built by someone who's actually lived through the problems it solves.

Outside of ShelfSpace, my family is everything to me. Being a dad is the most important role in my life — it's why I work so hard to build something meaningful. I want to help create an industry where good operators can make a real living and build something their families can be proud of. Every retailer and vendor we work with is someone's livelihood, someone's family. That's never lost on me.

Let's fix this industry together. I'm in it with you.

— Chris
