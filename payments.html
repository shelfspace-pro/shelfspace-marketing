Three ways money moves

E-check, paper check, or ACH. Same payment, your choice of delivery.

Whichever way it goes, the amount, the invoice it pays and the record in your books are the same.

Live today

E-check

A real Check 21 check, ready the moment you click Send. Your vendor downloads it, prints it on plain paper and deposits it from their phone. No check stock, no stamps, no trip to the post office.

  • Ready to deposit the same day
  • Mobile deposit, scanner or teller
  • Anyone can verify it at shelfspace.pro/verify
Live today

Mailed paper check

For the vendor who wants paper in hand. It's the same check, printed and mailed by us. You still just click Send.

  • Nothing to print or stuff on your end
  • Same check number, same record
  • Set per vendor, not all-or-nothing
Rolling out

ACH bank transfer

Money moves bank to bank, usually in 1–3 business days. Banks are connected with Plaid, so nobody types an account number and nobody's bank details sit in an email.

  • Bank connected with Plaid in about a minute
  • Nothing to print or deposit
  • E-check stays one tap away as a backup
For dispensaries
Paying your vendors

Check the boxes. Click Send. Every vendor is paid.

No check book, no printer, no stamps. Pick what to pay from your open payables and send it all at once.

1

Pick what to pay

Your open payables, sorted by how late they are. Tick the vendors you want to pay today and the total updates as you go.

2

Review and send

Each vendor gets paid the way they prefer. Change any row if you need to, send now or schedule it for later, and see the cash going out before you commit.

3

Your vendor hears about it right away

They get an email with the amount, the invoice it pays and simple steps to deposit. The check is in their portal, so you won't get a "where's my check?" call.

For brands & vendors
Getting paid by your stores

Your buyer pays from the email in under a minute.

No login, no account to create, no portal to learn. The pay link opens a single page with the amount already filled in.

1

The invoice lands with a Pay button

It comes from your business name, with the PDF attached. One button, with the exact amount on it.

2

Pay in full, pay part, or pay over time

Short on cash this week? They can pay part now or split it into installments you allow. Partial money in the bank beats a promise.

Paying by bank: one tap on Connect your bank with Plaid, log in to their bank, done. Nothing to type.

3

Sign, confirm, done

Their connected bank shows up by name. They type their name to sign, confirm once, and the payment is on its way to you.

4

Prefer a check? It's on the same page.

E-check works at every bank, today. They enter the account once, and next time they can load it from their saved info.
