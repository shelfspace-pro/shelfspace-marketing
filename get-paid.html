The questions vendors ask first

Does the retailer need a ShelfSpace account to pay me?

No. They pay from a single link in the reminder email — no login, no signup, no app to download. They enter their bank and routing number, sign on their phone, and submit. It's closer to a card checkout than an onboarding.

What does it cost me?

You keep 99% of every invoice you collect. A 1% fee, capped at $100, applies only to invoices that actually get collected — if nothing comes in, you owe nothing. The retailer pays exactly what they owe; the fee comes out of the collected amount, never on top.

Is this legal and safe in cannabis?

Yes. What gets generated is a real digital check drawn on the retailer's bank account, payable to you — the same check they'd write by hand, just digital. ShelfSpace never holds or moves the money, which is exactly why it works where card processors and payment apps get shut off.

How fast do I get paid?

As soon as the retailer clicks Pay Now and submits, a digital check is generated payable to you. You deposit it by phone or print it, the same as any other check. There's no processor in the middle holding funds for a release window.

What if the retailer still won't pay?

You're no worse off than today, and you've spent nothing — the fee only applies when you actually collect. The reminders run automatically so you're not the one chasing, and everything you're owed stays tracked in one place instead of a spreadsheet.
