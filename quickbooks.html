How it works

Connect once. Never re-enter an invoice again.

Three steps, and the first is the only one you do.

01
Connect QuickBooks

Sign in to QuickBooks Online and approve the connection. That’s the whole setup on your side.

02
Every invoice comes in

We import every open invoice and every customer from your QuickBooks automatically. Nothing to export, nothing to retype.

03
Payments sync both ways

A store pays in ShelfSpace or you record it in QuickBooks. Either way, both sides update on their own.

Two-way sync

It works in both directions. Automatically.

Most tools push data one way and leave you to fix the rest. ShelfSpace and QuickBooks keep each other current.
