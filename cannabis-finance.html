Frequently asked questions

What cannabis finance problems can ShelfSpace solve?

Bring us whatever is stuck: paying vendors, getting paid by stores, slow banking, reporting for a lender, records for your CPA, or a process your team runs by hand. We run accounts receivable, accounts payable, payments, credit recovery and consignment today, and if what you need doesn't exist yet, we build it.

How can you build something in days?

One team makes every decision and writes every line, with no roadmap queue and no ticket line. We build on a platform already connected to Metrc, QuickBooks, Xero and our payment rails, so a new tool starts with banking, payments and books already wired in.

Is ShelfSpace a bank?

No. We're the software and service that moves your money and keeps it organized. ACH transfers, mailed checks and e-checks go out through our banking partners, and you keep your own bank account.

Can ShelfSpace send payments for a cannabis business?

Yes. Pay vendors and get paid by stores from one screen, by ACH bank transfer, mailed paper check or e-check, with every payment tied to its invoice and your books.

How much does it cost?

Flat monthly price based on your brand's size, month-to-month, no contract. We'll scope anything custom on the call.
