What doesn't change

  • We never take a cut of your invoices. Not now, not ever. We're paid by the retailer. You aren't.
  • You own every credit memo decision. Yes, ShelfSpace surfaces credit opportunities from the retailer's data — returns, expirations, co-marketing. But every credit memo goes to you for approval. You can dispute or reject any of them. Nothing comes off your invoices without your sign-off.
  • You own the relationship with the retailer. We don't get in the middle of pricing, terms, or sales conversations — we just handle the payment side.
  • You set your own pricing. ShelfSpace doesn't price your products.
  • You set your own payment terms. COD, Net 7, Net 30, Net 60 — your call. Once an invoice hits its due date and is approved, the check is cut.
  • You can stop using ShelfSpace any time. No contract with you, no lock-in, no exit fee.
  • Your products, your branding, your sales conversations — unchanged. ShelfSpace is plumbing, not a marketplace.

The money questions

Does this cost me anything?

No. The portal is free, there are no per-transaction fees, and we don't take a percentage of your invoices. We're paid by the retailer, not by you. We've never charged vendors and have no plans to.

Do you need my bank account information?

No. ShelfSpace never collects vendor bank account numbers — there's no field for it in the system. You're paid with a Check 21 check you download from this portal and deposit like any other check. There's nothing stored for anyone to steal. If you want the longer version, here's our honest answer to whether ShelfSpace is safe for vendors.

When do I get paid?

On the terms you've already agreed to with the retailer. Wholesale invoices pay COD or on net terms (Net 7, Net 30, etc.) — once the invoice is approved and at its due date, the check is cut and you download it from the portal. If you ship on consignment, settlement runs weekly from POS data and your check is ready that cycle. Your terms don't change — we just stop being the reason you're waiting.

What if the retailer doesn't approve my invoice?

We're the AP team now, so the question comes to us first. We triage with Metrc data and delivery records, and most issues resolve within 24 hours without you chasing anyone. If we can't resolve it ourselves, we loop in the retailer directly — you're not stuck in a portal ticket queue waiting on someone to respond.

What kind of check is it? Will my bank accept it?

It's a Check 21–compliant Substitute Check, legally valid under federal law. You can deposit it by mobile phone, print it and walk it into the bank, or process it through remote deposit capture. Your bank doesn't treat it differently from a paper check from the retailer.

My settlement report says PAID EXTERNALLY on the last page instead of a check. What does that mean?

It means the retailer paid you outside ShelfSpace — using their own check, an ACH from their bank, or cash — instead of having the platform cut a Check 21. The settlement is resolved on the platform's side and the math is exactly what it would have been if they'd used the Check 21 path. You're not owed anything different. The stamp just confirms the payment was made off-platform. If you haven't received the corresponding payment from the retailer through whichever method they used, reach out to them directly with the settlement reference.

What happens if ShelfSpace goes away?

Your relationship with the retailer is unchanged. You'd go back to invoicing them directly, the way you did before. Nothing about your business depends on us continuing to exist — we don't hold your money, we don't hold your data, we don't lock you in.

Consignment

If you're being asked to ship on consignment, here's how it works.

Your product, your inventory, until it sells. When you ship on consignment, the product lives on the retailer's shelf but stays your property. The retailer's POS sees the unit ring at the register, and that's the trigger that creates the obligation to pay you. No invoice required up front. No COD. No Net 30 from a purchase order that may or may not exist.

Settlement runs weekly from POS data. Every cycle, the platform pulls the week's POS data and calculates what's owed to you on each unit per your wholesale terms with the retailer. You see the calculation line by line in the portal. Your check is ready that cycle. No invoicing, no chase, no reconciliation calls.

Unsold product comes back, no questions. If a SKU isn't moving, you can pull it. No write-off, no awkward "we need to return this" conversation. Your product, your decision.
