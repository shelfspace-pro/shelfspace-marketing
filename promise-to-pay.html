The Problem

A promise is easy to lose.

A store says “we’ll pay Friday.” Then Friday comes and goes, and nobody notices.

Promises get lost

It was said on the phone or buried in an email. It never landed anywhere your team will see it again.

Reminders keep going

Your reminder emails don’t know the store already said yes. So you nag a buyer who just committed to pay.

Missed dates go unseen

Nobody checks on Friday. By the time someone asks, the money is weeks late.

How it works

The store promises. ShelfCollect keeps track. You know the outcome.

01

The store promises

“We’ll pay 1041 and 1052 by Friday.” You log it in ShelfCollect: which invoices, how much, and by when.

02

Reminders pause

Reminder emails on those invoices stop until the promised date. No nagging a store that already committed.

03

ShelfCollect checks QuickBooks

Payments are checked automatically. No spreadsheet, and nobody has to remember.

04

You know if it’s kept

Every promise is marked Kept, Kept late, Partly paid, or Missed. Miss the date and you hear about it right away.

Less Work for Your Team

Promises get logged, even when you’re busy.

Your assistant logs it from the email

When a store replies to an invoice or a reminder, the reply goes to your AI assistant. If the store gives a payment date, the assistant records the promise and confirms it back to the store, with your sales rep copied.

Your reps log their own stores

Sales reps can log promises for the stores they own. Their Monday email shows which promises are coming due and which were missed, so they follow up while it still matters.

An AI call brings one back New

With AI Calls, an automated assistant phones the store and asks for an exact date and how they’ll pay. The promise comes to you; click Confirm and it’s tracked like any other.
