How It Works

How ShelfSpace manages your consignment program

We plug into your existing systems and handle everything. Nothing changes about how you receive product, stock shelves, or sell to customers. We just make sure vendors get paid and you keep your cash.

01

We connect to your POS and METRC

ShelfSpace integrates with Dutchie, Flowhub, and other cannabis POS systems. We pull sales data automatically. Your METRC manifests flow through as normal.

02

We onboard your vendors

We work directly with your vendors to set up consignment agreements. Fair terms, clear contracts, no awkward conversations for you.

03

We crunch the numbers weekly

Every week our engine calculates what sold, at what price, and what each vendor is owed. Accurate to the penny, backed by POS data.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do vendors get paid?

ShelfSpace issues Check 21 compliant checks to vendors every week, based on actual sales data pulled from your POS. Checks are printable on standard paper and mobile-deposit ready. Vendors also get a detailed settlement report showing every line item — what sold, at what price, and their split.

How much does consignment cost?

Consignment is included in the free 60-day pilot. For post-pilot pricing details, visit our pricing page.

Will my vendors agree to consignment?

Most do. Vendors get guaranteed weekly payments (no more chasing invoices), access to more shelf space, and a neutral third party managing the relationship. We handle the conversations and contracts — you don't have to pitch it yourself.

Does anything change about how I receive product?

No. Product is delivered, manifested in METRC, and received into your POS the same way it is today. Your staff, your workflow, your process — nothing changes. Customers can't tell the difference either.

What POS systems do you integrate with?

We currently integrate with Dutchie and Flowhub, with more integrations coming. If you use a different POS, reach out — we're adding new connections regularly.

What happens if a product doesn't sell?

That's the whole point. With consignment, unsold inventory is the vendor's risk, not yours. No markdowns eating your margin. No expired product you already paid for. If it doesn't sell, you haven't lost a dime.

Can I run consignment and wholesale at the same time?

Absolutely. Most of our retailers run both. ShelfSpace manages consignment settlements and wholesale AP payments through the same engine. One platform for every vendor, every payment.
