Accounts Payable

Nobody started a dispensary to do accounts payable.

ShelfSpace takes AP off your plate entirely. Every vendor gets paid on time and accurately. You just approve — we handle everything else.

The Reality

What AP looks like without ShelfSpace

The spreadsheet nightmare

Invoices in email, payments tracked in Excel, check stubs in a drawer. Nobody knows what's been paid, what's pending, or what's overdue.

Vendors calling every day

Where's my payment? Did you get my invoice? Can you resend the check? Your team spends hours every week fielding calls that have nothing to do with running a dispensary.

Money walking out the door

Paying for shorted deliveries nobody caught. Credits expiring because nobody tracked them. Double-paying invoices because there's no system. It adds up to tens of thousands a year.

We replace all of that with a team that runs your AP like a real finance department — for a fraction of the cost of hiring one.

Done For You

Here's what we take off your plate

01
We Handle This

We onboard your vendors

Give us your vendor list — cannabis or non-cannabis, it doesn't matter. We send invitations, set up portal accounts, and get every vendor payment-ready. No paperwork, no bank details needed upfront.

Pay anyone: Cannabis vendors, wholesale distributors, landlords, marketing agencies, consultants — if you write them a check, we handle it.
02
We Handle This

We verify before you pay

We match every invoice to what was actually received. If a delivery was short or an invoice doesn't match, you know before you cut the check — not after. You only pay for what you actually got.

Catch every discrepancy: Most operators unknowingly overpay vendors on shorted deliveries. We catch every discrepancy before a check goes out.
03
You Approve, We Execute

We process payments and cut checks

You review and approve. We generate Check 21-compliant checks, notify vendors, and sync every payment to QuickBooks. Vendors download checks instantly from their portal.

Check 21 Compliant: Every check is a valid Substitute Check under Federal Law. Print-at-home legal. Mobile deposit ready. Syncs to QuickBooks in real time.
04
We Handle This

We handle vendor questions

When vendors have questions about payments, invoices, or their account — they email us, not you. We manage every vendor inquiry so you never field another AP call.

We manage AP for a two-shop dispensary chain in Massachusetts — 40+ vendors, every payment on time, $45,000/year saved on AP and operations payroll.
