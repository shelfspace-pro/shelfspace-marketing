Step 7 / 10 · Vendor Decision

Vendors approve. Or open discussion.

One memo. Two answers. We handle whichever lands.

Done. Posted to your books. The vendor's next check is smaller by the credit amount.

Silence is not approval. The memo waits until the vendor responds. If they ask questions, we answer them. If they push back on a line, we negotiate that one line and approve the rest. The retailer doesn't sit in those emails.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What credits do you actually recover?

Three documented categories: product returns that physically shipped back to the vendor (Metrc transfer trail), expiration credits when product expired before sale, and co-marketing credits when in-store promos went uncredited. Every credit memo cites the line-item backup in Metrc and your POS.

Do my vendors have to agree to credits?

Yes. Every credit memo we send waits for the vendor to reply Approved or Let's discuss. We don't auto-apply on silence — we follow up every few business days until they respond, and we work through any line they want to question. Vendors prefer the structure because it's transparent: every line is cited to Metrc.

How quickly will I see results?

First credits identified within two weeks of data analysis. Vendor approvals follow over the next 30 days. Approved credits apply on the next settlement to that vendor.

How is this different from a collection agency?

We're not collecting debts. We're recovering credits you're already owed — returns, expirations, and co-marketing adjustments. Vendors prefer the structure because credit memos arrive with line-item Metrc backup, so there's documentation to confirm instead of invoicing back-and-forth.

What does credit recovery cost?

Commission-based on what we recover. No monthly fee. No recovery, no fee. Bundle with managed AP and the commission rate drops further. At our Massachusetts client, we identified unrecovered credits in the $8,000–$25,000/month range. See pricing.

Can I use credit recovery without your AP service?

Yes. Credit recovery is standalone. It pairs naturally with our managed AP if you want one team across vendor finances — same Metrc connection, same vendor relationships, lower commission rate when bundled.
