FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do my vendors have to agree to credits?

Yes, but we handle the entire approval process. We send the credit memo to the vendor, manage any questions or disputes, and track it to resolution. Vendors appreciate the structure — it's transparent and fair for both sides.

What if a vendor disputes a credit?

We manage the resolution directly with the vendor. Everything is tracked with a full audit trail — dates, amounts, communication history. You stay out of it.

Do I need to change my POS or METRC workflow?

No. We pull data from your existing systems. Nothing changes on your end — same POS, same METRC process, same receiving workflow.

What does credit recovery cost?

We only get paid when you get paid. If we don't recover credits, you owe nothing. Pricing is based on what we recover for you — most operators pay a fraction of the savings. Visit our pricing page for details.

How quickly will I see results?

Most operators see their first recovered credits within two weeks of data analysis. Vendor approvals typically follow within 30 days.

What if you don't recover anything?

You pay nothing. We only get paid when you get paid — that's the guarantee.

How is this different from a collection agency?

We're not collecting debts. We're recovering credits you're already owed under existing vendor agreements — returns, expirations, and co-marketing adjustments. These are standard business credits that vendors expect. We handle them professionally, and vendors actually prefer the structure because it's transparent and fair.
