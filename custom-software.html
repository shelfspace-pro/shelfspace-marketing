The problem

Off-the-shelf software stops where cannabis starts.

Most cannabis companies run on a stack of tools that don’t talk to each other, held together by spreadsheets and whoever knows where the numbers live.

The money part gets refused

Mainstream payment tools screen cannabis out. Brex’s prohibited activities list names marijuana-related activity, and BILL’s acceptable use policy bars plant-touching businesses.

Nothing connects

Your POS, Metrc, your books and your bank each hold part of the picture. Someone re-types it every week, and every re-type is a chance to get it wrong.

Generic developers start from zero

A dev shop that hasn’t built for cannabis has to learn Metrc, separate books for each license, 280E coding and banks that won’t touch the industry, on your dime.

Every project starts on a platform we already run for cannabis companies, so you pay for the part that’s unique to you, not for the plumbing.

FAQ

Custom software, answered.

What kind of software do you build for cannabis companies?

Anything that touches your money or your back office: expense and reimbursement tools, bill pay, collections and AR workflows, integrations between Metrc, your POS and your books, dashboards across several companies, and AI assistants that handle invoices and email.

Why not hire a regular software developer?

You can, but they start from zero on Metrc, separate books for each license, 280E coding and finding a payment provider that will work with cannabis. We’ve already built all of that, so your project pays for the part that’s unique to you.

Can the software pay people and vendors?

Yes. Payments are built into the platform: e-checks, mailed checks and bank transfers from your own account, with approvals before anything is sent and every payment recorded in Xero or QuickBooks.

Do you integrate with Metrc, Xero and QuickBooks?

Yes. ShelfSpace is a certified Metrc third-party vendor and connects to Xero, QuickBooks Online, Outlook and Gmail today. If you need a system we don’t connect to yet, we scope that integration as part of the project.

What does a custom project cost?

Each project is scoped on the first call and quoted once we agree what it should do. We build on our existing platform, so you aren’t paying to rebuild payments, logins or integrations that already exist.

Who hosts and supports it?

We do. Hosting, security, two-factor sign-in, updates and support are part of it, and when Metrc or your accounting software changes, we update the tool.
