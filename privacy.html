Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: September 24, 2026
Last Updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

ShelfSpace Technologies Inc. ("ShelfSpace," "we," "us," or "our") operates the ShelfSpace platform at ourshelf.space and the marketing website at shelfspace.pro (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our Service, including our accounts payable and payment services ("ShelfPay"), our accounts receivable services for vendors ("ShelfCollect"), and our AI assistant ("ShelfiQ").

This Policy covers information about the people who use the Service on behalf of retailers, vendors, and other businesses; visitors to our website; and individuals whose information we receive while providing the Service even though they do not use it themselves, such as trading-partner contacts, people who email us or reply to emails sent through the Service, business owners and control persons, businesses that owe money to a vendor using ShelfCollect ("Payers") and the people who act for them (see Section 3), and our customers' retail customers whose information appears in uploaded files. The Service is provided to businesses; information about a business (as opposed to an individual) is also governed by our Terms of Service.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account — first name, last name, email address, password, business name, business type, and state
  • Complete business verification and due diligence — legal business name, DBA name, business address, cannabis license number, Employer Identification Number (EIN), federal tax classification, ownership and control information (names, titles, ownership percentages, and other identifying details of owners, officers, and control persons), and, where required for payment services, government-issued identification of controlling owners and documents verifying your bank account
  • Authorize payment processing — bank name, routing number, account number, account type, and your authorized signature. By providing this information, you authorize ShelfSpace to issue payment instruments on your instructions (including remotely created checks, checks bearing your authorized signature, and, if and when made available, ACH entries) as described in our Terms of Service
  • Provide signatures — the authorized signature you draw or upload, which is printed on payment instruments issued on your instructions, and electronic signatures used to accept agreements and authorizations
  • Submit tax documentation — W-9 equivalent data including EIN and tax classification. For affiliate representatives, taxpayer identification information needed for tax reporting
  • Choose text-message security codes — your mobile phone number
  • Use the Platform — product listings, inventory data, sales transactions, settlement records, purchase orders, credit memos, invoices, receivables, promotions, and other business data you enter or upload
  • Upload data files — CSV and other files containing sales, inventory, returns, discounts, customer lists, and other transaction data
  • Upload business documents — invoice PDFs, purchase orders, delivery receipts, and other documents uploaded for AI-powered data extraction
  • Use a ShelfSpace-hosted email address — emails and attachments sent to the accounts payable or accounts receivable address ShelfSpace provides you, including from your vendors or customers, which may contain the names, email addresses, and business information of their personnel
  • Connect third-party integrations — API credentials for state track-and-trace systems (e.g., Metrc), OAuth tokens for accounting platforms (e.g., QuickBooks Online), and configuration settings for connected services; and, if you ask our personnel to work in your other business systems (such as a point-of-sale back office), the sign-in details you provide for that purpose
  • Use AI features — questions and conversations with the ShelfiQ AI assistant, including via the Platform, email, and the chat assistant on shelfspace.pro
  • Manage contacts — names, email addresses, phone numbers, and roles of key business contacts (owners, buyers, AP/AR contacts, sales representatives), including contacts at your trading partners and Payers
  • Contact us or request an evaluation — name, email address, business name, the data you provide for an evaluation, and any information you include in your communications

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access the Service, we may automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information — IP address, browser type, operating system, user agent string, and device identifiers
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • Authentication data — multi-factor authentication method, MFA enrollment status, login timestamps, and failed authentication attempts
  • Audit data — records of actions taken on the Platform, including data modifications, payment approvals, document generation, and agreement acceptance (with IP address and user agent for legal compliance)
  • Error and performance data — application errors, performance metrics, and diagnostic information collected through our error monitoring service, which may include information present when an error occurs
  • Email interaction data — whether emails we send are delivered, bounced, opened, or clicked, and unsubscribe requests
  • Analytics — in the Platform, privacy-focused performance analytics provided by our hosting provider; on shelfspace.pro, Google Analytics and Apollo.io website analytics, which use cookies and similar technologies to measure site usage and to identify the businesses that visit our website
  • Cookies and similar technologies — we use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, keep you signed in, protect against abuse, and improve user experience

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from the following sources:

  • State track-and-trace systems (Metrc) — inventory, transfer, manifest, sales, package, facility, and lab-result data, synced via API using credentials you provide
  • Accounting platforms (QuickBooks Online) — chart of accounts, vendor and customer records, bills, bill payments, vendor credits, invoices, payments, attachments, payment status, and bank-account transaction reports, synced in both directions via OAuth
  • Point-of-sale exports — sales, discount, return, and cost data from files you upload or that our personnel export at your request (we do not operate automated integrations with point-of-sale systems)
  • Your trading partners and our other customers — information about your business and your contacts that appears in invoices, statements, accounting records, contact lists, and emails they share with us
  • Public and government sources — state cannabis license registries and other state business and tax registries, U.S. government sanctions lists (including lists published by the U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control), public court records (including through CourtListener, operated by the Free Law Project), and news and other publicly available web sources
  • Banks and payment partners — payment status, check clearing, returns, and information they provide in connection with their own due diligence

2.4 Consumer Information in Uploaded Files

Files that our customers upload from point-of-sale systems may contain information about their retail customers, including, for medical sales, patient or caregiver identifiers, dates of birth, and contact details. We do not need or use this information. Our ingestion processes are designed to discard consumer-identifying fields rather than store them in our database records. The original files you upload are kept in access-controlled storage as part of your business records, and raw files uploaded for an evaluation are deleted as described in Section 10. If you are a consumer and believe your information was provided to us, please contact the business you purchased from, or contact us.

3. Payers and Other Non-Account Holders (ShelfCollect)

When a vendor uses ShelfCollect to bill and collect amounts owed to it, we process information about the businesses that owe the vendor money ("Payers") and the people who work for them, whether or not they have a ShelfSpace account. We process this information on the vendor's behalf and for our own legitimate purposes of operating, securing, and improving the Service.

3.1 What We Collect

  • Information vendors provide or connect — Payer business names, license numbers, addresses, billing and purchasing contacts (names, email addresses, phone numbers, and roles), invoice and line-item details, amounts owed, due dates, and payment history, which vendors enter, upload, or import from connected systems such as QuickBooks Online or a state track-and-trace system (e.g., Metrc). When a vendor connects a track-and-trace system, we use its outgoing transfer records to identify the licensed businesses it ships to, and we create records and payer profiles for those businesses on the Platform.
  • Information from other Users and public sources — business and billing-contact details that other Users who transact with the same Payer provide, and license and registration details from state cannabis licensing records.
  • Information you provide when you pay — the name you type as your signature, your email address, the bank name, routing number, and account number you enter, any comment or document you attach, and your authorization and attestations.
  • Information collected automatically when you pay or read our emails — IP address, browser and device information, a device identifier derived from browser and device characteristics (used to prevent fraud), the date and time of your authorization, page views of a payment page, and whether a ShelfCollect email was opened (using a small image in the email).
  • Your correspondence — the content of emails you send in reply to ShelfCollect messages, including attachments.
  • Saved payment information — if you choose "save my info," your bank details are stored in encrypted form, linked to your verified email address, so you can pay faster next time. You can stop us from using them at any time with the "forget" option on the payment page or by contacting us; after that they are no longer used for new payments and are retained, encrypted and with restricted access, only as described in Section 3.3.

3.2 How We Use and Share It

  • To provide ShelfCollect for the vendor — sending invoices, reminders, statements, and receipts to Payers on the vendor's behalf and in the vendor's name, hosting payment pages, and creating the payment instruments that Payers authorize
  • To protect payments — detecting unauthorized, duplicate, or self-dealing payments, and checking licensing, sanctions, and other compliance lists
  • To match records — matching invoices and Payers to track-and-trace shipment records and to the correct licensed business
  • To answer replies — responding to Payer replies using AI on the vendor's behalf (see Section 7), and routing disputes and other matters to the vendor or our personnel
  • To report status to the vendor — measuring whether ShelfCollect emails and payment pages are received and viewed, so vendors can see the status of their invoices
  • With the vendor you are paying or corresponding with — we share with the vendor the information about your payment and correspondence, including your name and signature, the payment instrument (which, like a paper check, displays the bank routing and account numbers you entered), payment status, email and page-view activity, comments, attachments, and any dispute you raise
  • Shared business records — business details and billing contacts for a Payer may be visible to other Users who transact with that same business, and may be used to send those Users' invoices and reminders to that business's billing contacts. We do not show one vendor the amounts another vendor is owed.
  • Anyone who has a payment link — payment links work without a login. Anyone who has a link can see the invoice information on that page, and link previews may show the vendor's name, the amount due, and whether it is past due.
  • Banks and service providers — the banks that process a payment instrument, and the service providers listed in Section 13, receive the information needed to do so.

3.3 Retention and Choices

ShelfCollect payment and authorization records (including banking information entered for a payment, your typed signature, IP address, and device information) are kept with the related transaction records for at least seven (7) years to document authorization and resolve disputes. Saved payment information is used until you remove it or the related installment plan ends; after that it is no longer used for new payments but may be retained, encrypted and with restricted access, for the same transaction-record period. Email-open and page-view records are kept while the related invoice is open and with its transaction records afterward.

If we hold information about you because a vendor uses ShelfCollect, you may contact us at chris@shelfspace.pro to request access, correction, or deletion, and we will respond as required by applicable law. Some information must be kept as part of the vendor's business records or to meet legal and payment-record requirements. Questions about what you owe should go to the vendor. You can stop ShelfCollect emails to your address using the unsubscribe link in any of them; this stops only that category of email to that address and does not change any amount owed. The terms that apply when you pay through a ShelfCollect payment page are in Section 6B.12 of our Terms of Service.

6. Compliance Reviews and Screening

To protect the Service and to meet our obligations and those of our banking partner, we review businesses that use our payment services, at onboarding and periodically after that. A review may include: checking cannabis licenses against state registries; screening the business and its owners, officers, and control persons against U.S. government sanctions lists; searching public court records for criminal, civil, and bankruptcy matters; searching news and other publicly available web sources, including through AI-assisted web searches; and assigning an internal risk rating. We keep the findings, their sources, and the decisions made.

  • Who decides. Automated screening may place a temporary hold on payments, for example on a possible sanctions match. A ShelfSpace team member reviews the findings, and a person, not an AI system, decides whether a hold based on a possible sanctions match or on review findings is lifted or kept. Some temporary holds end automatically when their stated period ends or when the condition that triggered them clears.
  • How results are used. Review results are used only to decide whether and how we provide payment services and other features to a business, to comply with law, and to manage fraud and compliance risk. They are shared with our banking partner, which may view them through a restricted interface for its own due-diligence and anti-money-laundering obligations, and with authorities where required. We do not sell review results or provide them to anyone for any other purpose.
  • Not consumer reports. We do not obtain consumer reports from consumer reporting agencies for these reviews, and review results are not used to make decisions about any individual's eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, or housing.
  • Your opportunity to respond. Where law permits, before a hold or refusal based on public-record, court-record, or news information about a business or one of its owners, officers, or control persons becomes final, we tell the business the general nature and source of that information and give the business and the affected individual an opportunity to dispute its accuracy or completeness. We correct our records if the information is shown to be inaccurate. An individual may also contact us at chris@shelfspace.pro to ask what information about them we hold from a review.

8. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest, with additional encryption of bank account numbers and integration credentials
  • Multi-factor authentication for user accounts
  • Row-level security so that users can access only their own organization's data, and role-based access controls for our personnel
  • Audit logs of changes to records, including financial and banking records, which users and our staff cannot edit or delete
  • Encrypted backups stored with a separate provider
  • Periodic review of our security practices

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Personnel access. Authorized ShelfSpace personnel may access information, including personal information, as needed to provide, support, secure, and investigate the Service, including by viewing or operating a customer's account as an administrator to provide support and managed services the customer requests, to investigate fraud or errors, and to meet legal obligations. Such access is subject to confidentiality obligations and is role-restricted, and actions our personnel take in a customer's account are logged. Access to credentials, encryption keys, and security codes is restricted.

Data Breach Notification. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you without undue delay and as required by applicable law. Notification will be provided via email to the address associated with your account and, where appropriate, through an in-platform notification. Breach notifications will describe, to the extent known and permitted by law, the nature of the breach, the types of information affected, steps we are taking in response, and steps you can take to protect yourself.

10. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and afterward for as long as needed for the purposes below. Because the Service keeps financial records that other parties rely on, we generally do not permanently erase records from our active database. Instead, when a record is deleted in the Service or an account is closed, we mark it as deleted and remove it from use; it is then retained, with access restricted, for the periods below.

  • Transaction, settlement, payment, credit memo, invoice, and receivable records, and uploaded business documents and data files — at least seven (7) years, for tax, financial record-keeping, and dispute purposes. These records are shared with, and relied on by, your trading partners.
  • Payment instruments and payment instructions — images of issued checks, remittance records, and instruction and approval logs: at least seven (7) years.
  • Agreement, signature, authorization, and consent records — for as long as the agreement or authorization could be relevant to a legal claim, and at least seven (7) years; banking and payment authorization records at least two (2) years after the authorization ends, as required by payment-network rules.
  • Audit logs — retained indefinitely as a record of changes.
  • Compliance review records (screenings, findings, sources, and decisions) — at least five (5) years after the review, or longer if our banking partner or law requires.
  • Identity and due-diligence documents — for the life of the payment relationship and at least five (5) years after it ends, or longer where required by law or our banking partner.
  • AI conversations and email correspondence — for the life of your account and the transaction-record period above, because they may form part of the record of a credit memo, payment, or dispute.
  • Account and contact information — for the life of the account and the transaction-record period, because it identifies the parties to retained records.
  • ShelfCollect records about Payers — as described in Section 3.3.
  • Evaluation uploads — raw files uploaded for an evaluation are deleted automatically, generally within thirty (30) days and sooner once the evaluation data has been processed. File names and other file details, and the evaluation results, may be kept.
  • Integration credentials — used only while the integration is active. When you disconnect an integration, we stop using its credentials and, where the provider supports it, ask the provider to revoke our access; the encrypted credentials may be kept with the connection record. You can also revoke access directly with the provider.
  • Email suppression records — kept indefinitely so that we continue to honor your unsubscribe request.
  • System and webhook logs — generally about thirty (30) days; logs of inbound email processing are kept with the related correspondence.
  • Backups — encrypted database backups are kept for up to thirty-five (35) days, and monthly backups for up to about thirteen (13) months, then deleted. Backup copies of stored documents and files are kept separately and may be retained for as long as the related records, or longer. Information deleted from the active database may remain in backups until they expire.
  • De-identified and aggregated data — may be retained indefinitely.
