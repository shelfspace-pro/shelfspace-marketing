What does this cost?

The evaluation is free. After that: Core AP is $1,800–$2,500/month per location (invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor payment terms, vendor payments, ShelfiQ access, QuickBooks sync) — consignment terms included. Credit recovery is success-based — 25% of what we recover, you keep 75%, no fee if we don't recover anything. Average evaluation finds $8,000–$25,000/month in unrecovered credits. Multi-location discount applies; 6+ locations are custom.

How fast will I see results?

First credits identified in 14 days. Consignment settlements within 30 days. Full AP operation running within 60 days.

How is this different from AP software?

We're not software. We're a team that does the work. You don't learn a dashboard or manage a system. We verify invoices, cut checks, recover credits, run settlements, and handle vendor emails. You just approve.

How does this work with my bookkeeper or accounting firm?

ShelfSpace handles AP — invoice processing, three-way matching, per-vendor terms, vendor payments, vendor calls, credit recovery. Your bookkeeper or firm keeps month-end close, bank reconciliations, payroll, journal entries, financial statements, tax prep, and 280E compliance. We sync to QuickBooks in real time. More for firms →

Does this actually pay for itself?

Most operators run net-positive within 90 days. Core AP at $1,800–$2,500/month per location is roughly equivalent to the bookkeeper hours you free up. Credit recovery is success-based at 25% — average evaluation finds $8,000–$25,000/month in unrecovered credits, so you keep $6,000–$19,000/month in net profit on top of the AP value.
