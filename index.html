FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you collect my money?

The same way a great AR department would, in your name. Every open invoice goes out with a one-click Pay Now link. Then every past-due store gets a steady schedule of reminders until it pays, plus a polite phone call or a one-page final notice for the old ones. We answer the store replies, and your reps get a list every Monday of who owes what. You never chase an invoice again.

How do stores pay me?

From the Pay Now link in the email: an ACH bank transfer or a digital check, straight from the store's own bank account. No login and no card fees. A store that can't pay it all at once can set up an interest-free payment plan. The money goes straight to you. We never hold it.

How much does it cost?

A flat monthly price set by the size of your brand, never a cut of what we collect. We quote it on a 30-minute call, after we've looked at your past-due invoices. And if we don't collect 25% of your past-due invoices in the first month, it's free.

How hard is it to get started?

Not hard at all. Simply upload an invoice and click “Collect”. That's it.

What are the next steps?

Book a 30-minute call with Chris. We'll look at your past-due invoices together, show you what's collectable, and give you a price. If it's a fit, you upload an invoice, click “Collect”, and we take it from there.
