FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does this cost?

Your price is set by your monthly sales, never by how many invoices or payments you run. We quote it after your free review, so you see what you're owed before you see a price. See how the value adds up.

What if it doesn't pay for itself?

Then we refund the difference. If the credits we recover for a dispensary, or the past-due payments we collect for a brand, come to less than you paid us in the first 90 days, we pay back the gap.

How fast will I see results?

Day 1 we review your last 90 days for free. First credits are found within 14 days, we're paying your vendors within 30, and the whole AP desk is running within 60.

Do you replace my buyer or bookkeeper?

No. Your buyer keeps product and vendor relationships. Your bookkeeper keeps close, payroll, tax, and 280E. We take the invoice and payment work off their plates and keep QuickBooks current, so their month-end gets faster.

Do you only work in Massachusetts?

No. We work in every state with Metrc. Massachusetts was first, with 50+ vendors already paid through ShelfSpace there.
