Your words, your decision

Say only what you'll really do.

Keep our wording or write your own, up to 1,200 characters. Pick 7, 10 or 14 days to pay. Then choose what happens if they still don't pay. Every box starts unchecked.

  • Put the store's account on credit hold.
  • Refer this balance to a collections agency or an attorney.
  • Report this unpaid balance to the state cannabis regulator.
  • Share this unpaid balance with industry payment-reporting groups.

FAQ

Straight answers

When can a brand send a final notice letter?

Once an invoice is past your mark: 60 days past due by default. Change the number in Payments → Settings → Collections → Final notice letters. Invoices that are disputed, paused, waiting on a promise to pay, on a payment plan, or already paid are never offered a letter.

Does a final notice letter go out automatically?

No. You send every letter yourself, from the invoice's row in AR Aging, the invoice page, or Review and send. You preview the letter and the email first, and what you preview is exactly what gets sent.

What does the final notice letter say?

It's one page on your letterhead: a scan-to-pay code and payment link at the upper right, the invoice details, a bold pay-by date, your own message, a 0% payment plan line when the invoice offers one, an “Already paid?” and “Think something is wrong?” line that invites a reply, and only the consequences you ticked. It's signed by your Accounts Receivable team.

Which consequences can the letter list?

Five, all off by default: stop shipping on credit terms, put the account on credit hold, refer the balance to a collections agency or an attorney, report the unpaid balance to the state cannabis regulator, and share the unpaid balance with industry payment-reporting groups. Tick any of them and a confirmation box asks you to confirm you'll follow through if it isn't paid by the date.

Do reminders stop after a final notice?

No. Reminders keep going as usual. The letter adds one clear, dated notice on top of them.

Is the final notice letter legal advice?

No. This letter isn't legal advice. If you're unsure about a consequence, check with your own attorney before you send.
