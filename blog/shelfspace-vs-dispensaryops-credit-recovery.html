DispensaryOps solves one problem. ShelfSpace solves all of them.

DispensaryOps — also known as Altura Dispensary Solutions — focuses on one thing: recovering vendor credits for cannabis dispensaries. They pull your data, identify eligible credits (returns, expirations, co-marketing), submit claims to vendors, and track the results. For $1,000 per month flat. That's a real service solving a real problem, and they deserve credit for carving out a niche that most operators overlook.

But credit recovery is one piece of a much bigger picture. After the credits are recovered, who's verifying the invoices? Who's catching duplicate payments? Who's settling consignment? Who's managing vendor communication? Who's processing the actual payments?

If you use DispensaryOps, you still need a separate AP solution for everything else. With ShelfSpace, it's all one team.

The credit recovery comparison

Let's be fair — DispensaryOps does credit recovery well. Their published stats: $5,059 per month average recovery per location, 406% ROI. That's solid, and it shows the problem is real. Dispensaries leave money on the table with vendors every single month.

ShelfSpace's credit recovery works the same way: we pull POS and Metrc data, build credit memos, submit to vendors, track approvals, and apply credits to settlements. We recently published a full case study walking through the exact workflow — from identifying the credit to applying it against open invoices.

The difference is in the pricing model. DispensaryOps charges $1,000 per month flat regardless of results. ShelfSpace charges a percentage of credits actually recovered — no recovery, no fee. If we don't find anything, you pay nothing. That alignment of incentives matters. You never pay for a month where nothing was recovered.

What happens after the credit is recovered?

This is the real gap. DispensaryOps hands you a recovered credit. Great. Now what?

With ShelfSpace, the recovered credit is immediately applied to the vendor's next open invoice. We calculate the net payment, cut the check, and sync it to QuickBooks. The credit goes from recovered to applied to paid to recorded in one cycle. No manual work. No spreadsheet tracking. No "I'll remember to deduct this next month."

We walk through this exact workflow in our vendor credit recovery case study — from the moment a credit is identified to the moment it's reflected in your books. When credit recovery lives inside the same system that manages your payments, credits never slip through the cracks.

The stack problem

If you use DispensaryOps for credit recovery, you still need:

  • Someone verifying invoices — or you're overpaying vendors on the invoices you do receive
  • A consignment settlement process — or you're doing it in spreadsheets and hoping both sides agree (we handle this)
  • A payment processing system — checks, ACH, or both (here's how Check 21 works)
  • Someone handling vendor emails and disputes — credit requests, payment inquiries, delivery confirmations
  • QuickBooks sync for everything — payments, credits, settlements all need to land in your books

That's four or five separate tools or people. ShelfSpace replaces the entire stack with one managed service. Credit recovery is one module in a system that also verifies invoices, settles consignment, processes payments, manages vendor communication, and syncs everything to your books.

Who should use what

Already have a robust AP operation and just need credit recovery? DispensaryOps might make sense. If you have someone verifying invoices, managing vendors, processing payments, and reconciling everything — and the only gap is credit recovery — a dedicated credit recovery service can fill that hole.

Need the full picture? Credits, invoices, consignment, payments, vendor management — ShelfSpace handles it all. One team, one system, one weekly approval. The credit recovery module is built into the same workflow that manages every other dollar you pay to vendors.

Frequently asked questions

How much does DispensaryOps cost vs ShelfSpace?

DispensaryOps charges $1,000/month flat for credit recovery only. ShelfSpace's credit recovery is percentage-based (no recovery, no fee). ShelfSpace also includes managed AP, consignment settlements, and vendor payments — DispensaryOps does not.

Can I use DispensaryOps for credit recovery and ShelfSpace for AP?

Technically yes, but it creates overlap and adds cost. ShelfSpace's credit recovery is built into the AP workflow — credits are recovered and applied to payments in the same system. Using a separate tool for credit recovery means manual reconciliation.

What types of credits does ShelfSpace recover?

Returns, expirations, and co-marketing/promotional credits. We calculate credits against keystone pricing, build documented memos, get vendor approval, and apply credits to the next settlement — all in one cycle.
