Xero Integration

Xero Accounts Receivable for Cannabis Brands: Collections That Post Back to Xero

If your books run on Xero, ShelfSpace connects to it directly. The invoices you raise in Xero come in on their own, we chase them with reminders, calls and a pay link, and every payment we collect posts back onto the invoice in Xero. Nobody retypes anything, in either direction.

At a glance

  • Invoices you approve in Xero, whether typed in by hand or pushed in by your ordering system, come into ShelfSpace on their own, with Xero’s own invoice PDF.
  • Reminders, calls and a pay link go out in your name. When Xero shows an invoice paid, credited or voided, reminders stop.
  • A payment we collect posts back to that invoice in Xero, into a clearing account, so the bank deposit matches.
  • Run several Xero organizations? Connect each one, then see and export them together or one at a time.

What the Xero connection does

It ties your Xero sales invoices to the collections work, both ways. Xero stays your books. ShelfSpace does the chasing and takes the payments, then writes the result back to Xero so your bookkeeper never has to enter a deposit by hand.

  • From Xero: approved sales invoices, the customers on them, the billing contacts, and any edit or payment your team makes in Xero.
  • To Xero: each payment we collect, recorded on the invoice it paid.
  • Never: new invoices or edits to yours. Invoices are created in Xero, by you or by the system that already feeds it.

How invoices get from Xero to your customers

Connect your Xero organization once, and the platform checks it for open, approved sales invoices around the clock. Each one lands in ShelfSpace ready to send, with the PDF Xero would have sent.

  • Customers become stores. A Xero customer is matched to the dispensary it belongs to, by license or by exact name and state. Anything less certain is a suggestion you confirm.
  • Billing contacts stay current. The customer’s email and any contact person you’ve set to receive invoices in Xero become the store’s billing list, and they update when you change them in Xero.
  • Edits follow. Change a due date or a line in Xero and the invoice here changes to match. Once a store has paid part of an invoice through ShelfSpace, an edit holds it for a look instead.
  • Your ordering system can keep feeding Xero. If your wholesale ordering platform pushes invoices into Xero, they come through here the same way.

Reminders that know when Xero says paid

The worst collections email is the one asking a store to pay an invoice it already paid. So Xero has the last word.

  • Paid in Xero, done here. When an invoice is paid, credited or voided in Xero, it closes in ShelfSpace and its reminders stop.
  • A deleted payment gets flagged. If someone removes a payment in Xero, the invoice holds with a “Check Xero” note and a to-do, instead of quietly starting the reminders again.
  • A lost connection pauses, not guesses. If Xero access drops, your admins hear about it once, and reminders on those invoices pause after 48 hours without a good read, until the connection is back.

Reminders, collection calls, payment plans and promises to pay all work on Xero invoices the same way they do everywhere else.

Payments post back to Xero

Stores pay from the link on the invoice, by check or bank transfer, in full, in part or on a payment plan. Before the platform takes a payment, it reads the invoice live from Xero, so nobody pays a balance that already changed.

When the money comes in, the platform records a payment on that invoice in Xero, dated the day it was issued, into a clearing account named ShelfSpace Collections Clearing (or an account you pick during setup). Your bookkeeper matches the bank deposit to that account, the same as any other payment. If a payment bounces, the platform takes it back off the invoice, or flags it for your bookkeeper if they already reconciled it.

Posting is on by default and can be switched off for any organization if your bookkeeper would rather record payments themselves.

Several Xero organizations, one view

Cannabis companies rarely have one set of books. Each license often means a separate company, and each company its own Xero organization.

  • Connect each organization. Each one stays separate. Its invoices, payments and posting never cross into another.
  • See them together. One company view shows what’s owed across every organization, with a filter for any one of them.
  • Decide who sees what. Access is set per person and per organization, so the Michigan team doesn’t see Oregon unless you say so.
  • Export for the close. Download open invoices and an aging summary for one organization or all of them together.

Your Xero data and AI

ShelfSpace uses AI to answer billing questions from stores and to help with collections. It reads an organization’s Xero data only after someone at your company agrees to it when connecting, and the consent you record says the data is never used to train an AI model.

Expense reports post to Xero too

The same connection carries expense reports. Employees snap receipts on their phones, approvers sign off, and each approved report posts to Xero as a bill to the employee with the receipts attached. Xero is retiring its Classic Expenses, and our guide covers what that changes.

Xero or QuickBooks: it works with both

ShelfSpace connects directly to Xero and to QuickBooks Online. Pick whichever your bookkeeper prefers. Collections, payments and expense reports work the same way on either, and a company with some organizations on each can run them side by side. Getting set up

Connect your Xero organization from Settings, pick it from the list Xero shows you, and the setup walks through your customers, billing contacts and where payments should post. Or hand it to us: we set it up and run the collections for you.

Frequently asked questions

Does ShelfSpace integrate with Xero?

Yes. ShelfSpace connects directly to Xero. It imports your approved sales invoices and customers, follows edits and payments made in Xero, and posts the payments it collects back to the invoice in Xero.

Does ShelfSpace create invoices in Xero?

No. Invoices are created in Xero, by your team or by the ordering system that already feeds it. ShelfSpace sends them, follows them up and collects on them.

Where do collected payments post in Xero?

Each payment is recorded on the invoice it paid, into a clearing account called ShelfSpace Collections Clearing or an account you choose, so your bookkeeper can match it to the bank deposit. Posting can be switched off for any organization.

What happens if an invoice is paid directly in Xero?

It closes in ShelfSpace on the next read and its reminders stop. If a payment is later removed in Xero, the invoice holds with a note to check Xero instead of restarting reminders.

Can ShelfSpace handle several Xero organizations?

Yes. Connect each organization. Each stays separate, and a company view shows them together with a filter by organization, access set per person, and exports for one organization or all of them.

Does ShelfSpace work with QuickBooks too?

Yes. ShelfSpace connects directly to QuickBooks Online as well, and collections, payments and expense reports work the same way on either.
