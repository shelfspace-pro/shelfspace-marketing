Every Sales Rep Gets Their Collections List Every Monday

Your reps own the relationships. Now each one starts the week with every open invoice on their accounts, oldest first, and a one-tap way to text the buyer a pay link.

At a glance

  • Every Monday at 7 AM, each sales rep gets one email with every open invoice for the accounts assigned to them, including ones not due yet.
  • Each invoice has Text, Email, and View. Text opens the rep's own phone with the message and pay link already written.
  • No login for the rep or the buyer. The buyer taps the link and pays on one screen.
  • Add reps and assign accounts at Settings › Sales Reps: by account, by rep, in bulk, or from your spreadsheet.
  • Accounts with no rep go to your AR contact, and AR Aging now filters by rep.

Here's how cannabis collections usually work at a brand. The office runs an aging report. Someone exports it, sorts it by rep, and emails each rep a spreadsheet. The reps are on the road. The spreadsheet sits in an inbox. Two weeks later the same invoices are still open, and now they're a month older.

The frustrating part is that the rep is the best collector you have. The buyer knows them. The buyer answers their texts. A dispensary will ignore a third notice from an accounting address and pay the same day when their rep says "hey, can you knock out #1043 for me?" The rep just never had the list, or the link, in the moment they were talking to the buyer.

So we built the weekly sales rep collections email. It's part of ShelfCollect, and it turns every rep into a collector without adding a single task to their week.

What lands in each rep's inbox on Monday

One email per rep, every Monday at 7 AM their time. It opens with four numbers: total owed, the amount 31+ days late, the number of invoices, and the number of accounts. Then every open invoice on their accounts, grouped by how late it is and sorted oldest first: 91+ days, 61–90, 31–60, 1–30, then the ones that aren't due yet.

The not-yet-due invoices matter more than they look. A rep walking into a store on Tuesday can say "you've got one due Friday too" and clear two invoices in one conversation. That's how you stop invoices from going late in the first place.

One tap to ask for the money

Every invoice row has three buttons. This is the part reps love, because it takes the awkward out of asking.

1
Text
Opens Messages on the rep's phone with a short note already written: the invoice number, the amount, when it was due, a link to the invoice PDF, and the pay link. The rep picks the buyer from their own contacts and hits send. It comes from the rep's number, not from us.
2
Email
Opens a ready-to-send email already addressed to that account's billing contacts, with the same invoice PDF and pay link inside.
3
View
Opens the invoice so the rep can check it before reaching out. A rep looking doesn't count as the buyer opening it, so your collections tracking stays honest.

The buyer taps the pay link and lands on a single pay page. No account to create, no password, no app. They pay from their own bank, and the money comes to you. It never passes through us. If you want the details on that part, read how ShelfCollect collects on overdue invoices.

Nobody logs in to anything. Not the rep reading the email, not the buyer paying it. Everything works from a phone.

Set up your reps and their accounts in minutes

The email is only as good as the answer to "whose account is this?" That lives at Settings › Sales Reps in your vendor portal, and it's built for a real sales team, not a demo:

  • Add your reps. Name, email, phone, title, and the states they cover.
  • Assign accounts by account. Every dispensary you sell to, one rep dropdown per row. Unassigned accounts float to the top so you see the gaps first. Search, filter by rep, or select a batch and move them all at once.
  • Or assign by rep. Open a rep, see their book, and hand them more accounts.
  • Or import your spreadsheet. Most brands already track this in Excel. Upload it and the platform matches each account, adds any new reps, and shows you every change before anything is saved.

One rep per account keeps it simple. Reassigning a territory is a dropdown change, and next Monday's emails follow it.

No account falls through the cracks

Every sales team has accounts nobody owns: a house account, a store from a rep who left, a new buyer that hasn't been assigned yet. Those don't disappear. Any account without a rep goes into one email to your AR contact, with the same one-tap buttons. The same happens if a rep has no working email or opts out. Someone always has the list.

Run your Monday sales meeting from AR Aging

Your AR Aging screen now has a sales rep filter. Pick a rep and the aging buckets and invoice list narrow to their accounts. Pick Unassigned to see what nobody owns. It turns the Monday sales meeting into a quick walk down the list, rep by rep, instead of a spreadsheet hunt. You stay in control of what goes out

Before a single rep gets an email, you can see exactly what they'll see. The Weekly collections digest card in Settings › Sales Reps lets you:

  • Turn the weekly email on or off for your company
  • Preview the email as any rep
  • Send yourself a test copy
  • Switch it off for one rep, or turn a rep back on
  • Choose whether unassigned accounts go to your AR contact

The email carries your logo and colors, so reps recognize it as yours. Replies go to your AR inbox. The step-by-step setup is in the weekly sales rep collections email doc and the sales rep assignments doc.

Why this changes collections for cannabis brands

Collections at a cannabis brand has always had a gap between the people who know what's owed and the people who can get it paid. Accounting has the numbers. Sales has the relationships. The weekly rep email closes that gap every Monday: the right person, the right list, and a link the buyer can pay in a minute.

The rep who made the sale is the rep who gets it paid. They just needed the list and the link in their pocket.

If you sell into cannabis and your reps have never seen the AR report, this is the fastest way we know to shrink it.

Frequently asked questions

When does the weekly sales rep email go out?

Every Monday at 7 AM in your company's local time. Each rep gets one email covering every open invoice on the accounts assigned to them, including invoices that aren't due yet.

Do my reps need to log in to ShelfSpace?

No. The email works on its own. Reps tap Text, Email, or View right from their inbox. The buyer doesn't need an account either. The pay link opens a one-screen pay page.

Does ShelfSpace text my customers?

No. The Text button opens the rep's own Messages app with the note already written. The rep chooses the buyer from their own contacts and sends it from their own number.

What happens to accounts that don't have a rep?

They go into one email to your AR contact, with the same one-tap buttons. The same applies if a rep has no working email or opts out, so no account goes uncovered.

Can I import my rep assignments from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload an Excel or CSV file at Settings › Sales Reps. The platform matches each account, adds any new reps by email, and shows you a preview of every change before you apply it.

Can I see what a rep will get before it sends?

Yes. Preview the email as any rep, or send yourself a test copy from the Weekly collections digest card in Settings › Sales Reps.
