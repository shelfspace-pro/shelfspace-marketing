Why this matters for a dispensary

Cannabis back-office work lives in too many places. Invoices come in by email. The real record of what arrived is in Metrc. The books are in QuickBooks. Credits live in a spreadsheet somebody keeps meaning to update. Getting a straight answer to “what do we owe Verdant Hill, and is it right?” can take an afternoon of tabs and exports.

ShelfSpace already pulls all of that into one place. Now your AI can reach it too. The question that took an afternoon takes one sentence, and the follow-up (“ask them for a corrected invoice”) takes another.

Everything you can do in ShelfSpace, you can do from a chat. You don't have to log in, click through screens, or export anything to Excel.

If you run a dispensary (ShelfPay)

  • Pay vendors. See what's due, what's ready and what's blocked, line up the week's payments, and send them within limits your company sets.
  • Match invoices to Metrc. Pull any invoice with its Metrc manifest, line by line, and get a plain-English reason for every mismatch.
  • Run credit recovery. Returns, destroyed product and price drops on each vendor's monthly credit memo, plus who has approved and who is pushing back.
  • Keep QuickBooks caught up. What synced, what's waiting and why, so a stuck bill gets fixed before your bookkeeper finds it.
  • Write to vendors. Ask for a corrected invoice, a missing copy or a credit, with the numbers already in the note.
  • Upload invoices and documents. Hand your AI a photo or PDF and it lands in the right store for review.
  • Settle consignment. Weekly settlements, splits and returns, explained line by line.

If you run a brand (ShelfSpace)

Brands get the other side of the same conversation. Your AI sees every invoice you've sent, who has paid and who hasn't. No Metrc connection required.

How to connect, in three steps

1

Add ShelfSpace to your AI

In ChatGPT or Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, add a custom connector, and paste https://ourshelf.space/api/mcp. In Meta Muse, register an app in ShelfSpace under Settings, AI agents, and paste its Client ID and Secret.

2

Sign in and pick your stores

Your normal ShelfSpace login and verification code, one time. Choose the stores your AI may use, then click Allow.

3

Start asking

Try “Which of my stores can you see?” Then ask the question you actually have.

Safe enough for money

Connecting an AI to your finances should make you ask hard questions. Here are the answers:

  • It signs in as you. No shared password, and it never holds a database key.
  • It sees only the stores you pick. Every other store stays invisible to it.
  • It gets your role and nothing more. A read-only person gets a read-only AI.
  • Payments follow your rules. A person confirms each payment, or your AI pays within per-payment and per-day limits you set. Bank-detail changes always need a person.
  • Everything is on the record. Every action is logged as done by your AI, and your team sees the badge in ShelfSpace.
  • Off in one click. Disconnect it in Settings and it loses access immediately.

For the technical reader

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard AI assistants use to reach outside tools. ShelfSpace implements it directly, and exposes the same tools as a plain web API, so there is one pipeline with two front doors.

MCP serverhttps://ourshelf.space/api/mcp, Streamable HTTP
AuthOAuth 2.1, authorization code + PKCE (S256), refresh tokens
DiscoveryRFC 8414 and RFC 9728 well-known metadata
ClientsDynamic registration (RFC 7591), client ID metadata documents, or a confidential app registered in Settings
Scopesshelfspace.read, shelfspace.act
RESTPOST /api/agent/v1/tools/{tool} with an OpenAPI 3.1 file at /api/agent/v1/openapi.json

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for cannabis brands and vendors?

Yes. Brands on ShelfSpace connect the same way. Your AI can see who owes you and how late, send invoices, statements and reminders, and track promises to pay. No Metrc connection required.

Is there an MCP server for cannabis accounting?

Yes. ShelfSpace runs a remote MCP server for cannabis back-office finance at https://ourshelf.space/api/mcp. Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Meta Muse or any MCP-compatible client and your AI can work accounts payable, receivables, Metrc invoice matching, QuickBooks, credit recovery, consignment settlements and vendor payments for the stores you choose.

Can ChatGPT connect to my dispensary's books?

Yes, through ShelfSpace. Add ShelfSpace as a custom connector in ChatGPT, sign in once with your ShelfSpace login, pick your stores and click Allow. ChatGPT can then answer from your real invoices, payments and Metrc data.

Can Claude check my invoices against Metrc?

Yes. With ShelfSpace connected, Claude can pull any invoice with its Metrc manifest match, line by line, and tell you which lines are short, over, missing from the manifest or billed twice.

Can an AI pay my cannabis vendors?

Yes, once your company turns on AI payments in ShelfSpace. You pick whether a person confirms each payment or the AI pays within per-payment and per-day limits you set. Bank-detail changes always need a person to confirm.

Do I have to log in to ShelfSpace every time?

No. You sign in once to connect your AI. After that you work from your AI's chat window.

Is it secure?

Your AI signs in as you, sees only the stores you pick, gets your role and nothing more, never holds a database password, and every action is logged as done by your AI. You can disconnect it in one click.
