Your Sales Reps Can Collect Right From Their Phone

Your reps already walk into every store they sell to. Now they can see what each store owes and ask for it, in one tap, while they're standing at the counter.

At a glance

▶ Watch the full tutorial with transcript →

  • Each rep signs in and sees only the stores assigned to them, never another rep's customers.
  • Two numbers up top: everything those stores owe, and the part that's late.
  • Filter by how late (Late, Not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91+, Promised), or search a store by name.
  • Tap any invoice to send a reminder, text a pay link, mark it paid, log a promise to pay, or have the AI assistant call.
  • Reps can't write off or delete anything. That stays with your admins.

Your sales reps are in a store every week. The buyer knows them and answers their texts. But when it comes to getting paid, most reps have no idea what that store owes. The office has the aging report. The rep has a handshake.

So each rep now gets their own view of ShelfSpace, built for the phone in their pocket. It shows the stores they cover, what's late, and every way to follow up, one tap away.

Only their stores, nobody else's

Each rep signs in with their own login. They see the stores assigned to them and nothing else. The totals, the list, the search and every button only reach their own customers. You assign the stores at Settings › Sales Reps.

What's owed and what's late, at a glance

Two numbers sit at the top. Owed to you is everything those stores haven't paid yet. Late is the part that's past its due date, with how many invoices that is.

Tap Late and the list drops to just the late invoices, with the most overdue on top. Each card shows the store, the amount, and how many days late it is. An Opens tag means someone at the store opened the invoice email, so they've seen the bill.

The rep's late list, on a phone. Shown with demo stores and amounts.

Walking into a store? Type its name in the search box, and only that store's invoices are left.

Every follow-up is one tap away

Tap any invoice and a sheet slides up with everything a rep can do about it:

  • Send reminder emails the invoice and its pay link again.
  • Share pay link opens the rep's own texts or messages with the link ready, so the buyer can pay right there.
  • Mark as paid is for when the rep is handed a check, so nobody chases a bill that's already been paid.
  • Log a promise to pay records the date the buyer gave.
  • Call with AI has the assistant phone the store about the bill.
  • Send final notice starts a formal letter you review before it goes out.
  • Open invoice shows the full details, contacts and history.

Tap an invoice, and every follow-up is right there.

When the buyer says "Friday"

Buyers make promises at the counter all the time. Now the rep can write it down. Pick the day and how the buyer told them, and the automatic reminders on that invoice pause until then. The buyer's pay link keeps working the whole time.

The card now says it's on hold and the date it was promised, so anyone on your team who opens it knows the plan. If the money doesn't come, the promise is flagged so a person decides what happens next. Nothing goes out to the store on its own.

The card after the rep logs a promise.

Nothing they can break

Reps can't write off an invoice, delete one, set up a payment plan or send an account to collections. Those stay with your admins. Reps do the part they're best at: asking the buyer they already know.

Setting up reps and assigning stores is covered in the sales rep assignments guide. Reps also get a collections list by email every Monday.

Frequently asked questions

Can a sales rep see other reps' customers?

No. A rep's login shows only the stores assigned to them. Totals, the late list, search and every action are limited to those stores.

What can a rep do from their phone?

Send a reminder email with the pay link, share the pay link by text, mark an invoice paid when they're handed a check, log a promise to pay, have the AI assistant call the store, start a final notice letter for you to review, or open the full invoice.

What happens when a rep logs a promise to pay?

The automatic reminders on those invoices pause until the date the buyer gave. The store's pay link keeps working. If the money doesn't arrive, the promise is flagged so a person decides what happens next. Nothing goes out to the store on its own.

Can a rep write off or delete an invoice?

No. Write-offs, deletes, payment plans and collections stay with your admins.

Does the rep need an app?

No. Reps sign in to ShelfSpace in their phone's browser. The screens are built for a phone.
