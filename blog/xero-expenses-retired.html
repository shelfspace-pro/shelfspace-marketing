Xero Expenses Guide

Xero Classic Expenses Is Retiring: How to Reimburse Employees in Xero Now

Xero is retiring Classic Expenses and switching off the ExpenseClaims API that older expense tools used. The way forward is the one most tools already use: every approved claim becomes a bill to the employee, paid from your bank. Here's how that works and how to choose a tool around it.

At a glance

  • Xero's developer changelog entry of 07/31/2025 says it's retiring Classic Expenses, disabling the already deprecated ExpenseClaims endpoint and deprecating the Receipts endpoint, under a Feb 2027 label.
  • Xero's current Expenses product turns each approved claim into a bill you pay like any other, or reimburses it through Xero Payroll, and it's included on the Established plan.
  • Outside tools land in the same place: Expensify exports out-of-pocket expenses as purchase bills, and Ramp creates reimbursements as bills with the employee as the vendor.
  • Out-of-pocket spend is a bill to the employee. Company card spend is a bank transaction. Mixing them up double-counts.

Sources checked 11/05/2026. Xero changes its plans and products, so confirm what your own organization shows.

If your team still files expense claims in Xero's classic screens, or uses a tool that reads them through Xero's ExpenseClaims API, that path is closing. You don't lose the ability to reimburse people. You lose one particular way of recording it, and the replacement is simpler: a bill.

What Xero is retiring

Xero is retiring the Classic Expenses product and the API behind it. Its developer changelog entry of 07/31/2025, titled "Classic Expenses API Deprecation," says Xero will be "disabling access to the already deprecated ExpenseClaims endpoint" and "will also be deprecating the Receipts endpoint." The entry carries a Feb 2027 label and doesn't give separate dates for each step.

Two groups feel it. Anyone still entering claims in the classic screens has to move to Xero Expenses or another tool. And any integration that writes expense claims or receipts through those endpoints has to post a different way. If a tool you rely on still uses them, ask the vendor what it does instead.

How expense claims post to Xero now

Expense claims post to Xero as bills now, payable to the employee. Xero's own product works that way: its Expenses page says "the approved claim becomes a bill you pay like any other." The bigger outside tools do the same.

The split matters. An out-of-pocket expense is money the company owes a person, so it belongs in accounts payable until it's paid. A card purchase is money that already left a company account, so it's a bank transaction. Post a card purchase as a bill to the employee and you'll pay them for something they never paid for.

How to reimburse an employee in Xero by hand

  • Set up the employee as a contact. One contact per person, named so nobody confuses them with a supplier.
  • Enter one bill per claim. A line per expense, each coded to the right account, with the merchant and purpose in the description.
  • Attach the receipts. The bill should carry its own evidence for your bookkeeper and your auditor.
  • Approve it. Someone other than the employee checks the business reason and the coding.
  • Pay it and record the payment. Record the payment against the bill from the bank account the money left, so the bank feed line matches.

That works for a handful of claims a month. It breaks down when dozens of people send photos by text, half the receipts arrive after the month closes, and two people submit the same lunch.

Xero Expenses vs Expensify and Ramp

The choice comes down to where people capture receipts, who approves, and how the money reaches the employee.

  • Xero Expenses keeps everything in Xero. The Expenses page says employees snap receipts in the Xero Accounting app and submit through Xero Me, and that it's included on the Established plan.
  • Expensify posts to Xero as purchase bills and lets you choose the bill status on export, per its Xero guide. With auto-sync on, it says reports export after approval.
  • Ramp creates the bill and, when it pays the employee by ACH, syncs the bill payment too, per its Xero overview. A reimbursement set to Manual Pay stays unpaid in Xero, with no payment recorded.

Cannabis companies have one more filter: whether the tool will move money for them at all. Check the provider's eligibility rules before you plan your reimbursements around its payments.

When to post: at approval or at payment

Ask your bookkeeper which they want. Posting at approval puts what you owe in accounts payable for the period it was approved, even if the reimbursement goes out after month end. Posting at payment keeps unpaid claims out of the books until the money moves. Either way, the bill and the payment should land once, with a reference that ties them back to the claim, so nobody enters the same report twice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Xero Classic Expenses being retired?

Yes. Xero's developer changelog entry of 07/31/2025 says that as part of efforts to retire the Classic Expenses product, Xero will disable access to the already deprecated ExpenseClaims endpoint and deprecate the Receipts endpoint, under a Feb 2027 label.

How do you reimburse an employee's expenses in Xero?

Enter the claim as a bill with the employee as the contact, attach the receipts, approve it, then record the payment from the bank account you paid it from. Xero's own Expenses product does the same thing: an approved claim becomes a bill you pay like any other, or you reimburse it through Xero Payroll.

Is Xero Expenses free?

Xero's Expenses page says it's included on the Established plan. Check that page for your region and plan before you count on it.

What's the difference between a bill and a spend money transaction in Xero?

A bill records money you owe someone and sits in accounts payable until you pay it, which fits an employee waiting to be reimbursed. A spend money or bank transaction records money that already left a company account, which fits a company card purchase.

How does Expensify post expenses to Xero?

Expensify's Xero guide says out-of-pocket expenses export as purchase bills, with a status of Draft, Awaiting Approval or Awaiting Payment, and company card expenses export as bank transactions.

Should expense claims post to Xero when approved or when paid?

That's a choice for your bookkeeper. Posting at approval puts the amount owed in accounts payable for the right period, even if the reimbursement goes out later. Posting at payment keeps unpaid claims off the books until the money moves.
