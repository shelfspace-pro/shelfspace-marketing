ShelfSpace vs Canix: an ERP and collections software, compared

Canix runs your inventory, compliance and production. ShelfSpace gets the invoices paid. Here is what each one does, from their own pages, and when you'd pick one, the other or both.

By Chris Mitchem · 11/02/2026 · Competitor facts are from each company's public pages as of 11/02/2026.

At a glance

  • Canix describes itself as an ERP for cannabis cultivators, manufacturers and distributors, with inventory, compliance, production and invoicing modules.
  • ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link.
  • Canix's public pages show invoicing, ACH payment links, payment tracking, A/R tracking and accounting integrations. They don't describe past-due reminders, calls or payment plans.
  • Pick Canix to run operations. Pick ShelfSpace to collect. Brands that need both can use both, with QuickBooks in the middle.

What Canix is

Canix calls itself "the best-in-class ERP for cannabis cultivators, manufacturers, and distributors" (Canix). An ERP is the system of record for inventory, production, orders and compliance. Canix's home page lists modules for inventory management, compliance, invoicing and purchase orders, task management, yield tracking, cultivation forecasting, labor costing and business intelligence reporting, plus a web app and an offline-enabled mobile app.

On the money side, Canix's invoicing module lets you "generate and record invoices for your sales orders," and its sales section says you can "create invoices, track payments, and analyze revenue" (Canix). Its finance page says you can "manage outstanding balances, track accounts receivable and accounts payable all from Canix" (Canix finance and accounting). Canix lists QuickBooks, Sage and LeafLink among its accounting integrations, and it accepts ACH payments through a payment portal or an invoice link (Canix blog).

What ShelfSpace is

ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link. We offer 0% payment plans to stores that need them, collect promises to pay, and send final notice letters when an invoice is badly late. Stores pay by e-check or bank transfer from the pay link, and payments land in QuickBooks.

ShelfSpace is not an ERP. It doesn't track inventory, plan cultivation or production, or file Metrc transfers for your operation. Running a dispensary rather than a supply chain? Here is what a store actually needs instead of a cannabis ERP.

Where Canix fits better

If your problem is running the operation, Canix is the better fit. A cultivator, manufacturer or distributor that needs one system for inventory, compliance, yields, labor costs and order-to-invoice needs an ERP, and ShelfSpace doesn't do that work.

Where ShelfSpace fits better

If your problem is that stores pay late, an ERP's A/R view tells you who owes you, but someone still has to chase them. That is the work ShelfSpace does for you, in your name, every week. If your Canix invoices already reach QuickBooks, ShelfSpace can pick up the open ones from QuickBooks, so the two don't compete.

Frequently asked questions

Is ShelfSpace a Canix alternative?

Only for the getting-paid part. Canix is an ERP that runs inventory, compliance, production and sales orders. ShelfSpace is collections software that sends invoices, follows up on past-due stores and takes payment. A brand can need the first and still have the second problem.

Can I use Canix and ShelfSpace together?

Yes. Canix lists QuickBooks among its accounting integrations, and ShelfSpace imports open invoices from QuickBooks, then follows up on them and records payments back.

Does Canix send past-due reminders?

We didn't find past-due reminders, collection calls or payment plans described on Canix's public pages as of 11/02/2026. Ask Canix directly for the current feature list.

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Flat monthly price based on your brand's size, month-to-month, no contract. We go over your price on a call.
