ShelfSpace vs LeafLink: marketplace payments vs collections

LeafLink is where many cannabis orders get placed, and it has its own payment tools. ShelfSpace is what you use when invoices go unpaid. Here is how they differ for a brand that wants to get paid.

By Chris Mitchem · 10/29/2026 · Competitor facts are from each company's public pages as of 10/29/2026.

At a glance

  • LeafLink calls itself the cannabis wholesale marketplace built for the entire supply chain, available in 30 markets.
  • Its Payment Suite pairs paid-on-sell-through with Direct Pay, which LeafLink says accepts ACH "with no processing fees."
  • ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link, whether the order came through LeafLink, a sales rep or an email.
  • If your orders run through LeafLink's marketplace, its payments sit right next to the order. If stores still pay late, ShelfSpace does the follow-up.

What LeafLink is

LeafLink describes itself as "The Cannabis Wholesale Marketplace Built for the Entire Supply Chain," available in 30 markets (LeafLink). Brands and distributors list products, and retailers place orders there.

For payments, LeafLink announced its Payment Suite, "PoST + Direct Pay, now included in your subscription" (LeafLink, 04/25/2025). PoST means paid on sell-through. The same post says Direct Pay lets you accept ACH payments through LeafLink "with no processing fees." Retailers get three options: pay now, pay later or pay on sell-through (LeafLink for retailers), and brands can track orders and payment status in one place (LeafLink for brands). QuickBooks is listed among its accounting integrations (LeafLink integrations).

What ShelfSpace is

ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link. It works on every open invoice you have, not only marketplace orders: we import them from QuickBooks and follow up with reminders, phone calls, payment plans and final notices until the store pays.

Where LeafLink fits better

If you sell through LeafLink's marketplace and want ordering, payment and sell-through payouts in the same place as the order, LeafLink is built for that. ShelfSpace is not a marketplace and won't bring you new buyers.

Where ShelfSpace fits better

A payment option only helps if the store uses it. When an invoice is past due, someone has to remind the store, call the buyer, agree a date or set up a plan, every week, until it is paid. ShelfSpace does that for you, across every invoice, including orders that never touched a marketplace.

Frequently asked questions

Is ShelfSpace a LeafLink alternative?

Not for ordering. LeafLink is a wholesale marketplace. ShelfSpace is collections software for the invoices stores haven't paid. Many brands could use LeafLink to take orders and ShelfSpace to collect on what is late.

Does LeafLink charge fees for payments?

LeafLink's 04/25/2025 announcement says its Payment Suite is included in the subscription and that Direct Pay accepts ACH with no processing fees. Brand subscription pricing is quoted by LeafLink's sales team.

Can ShelfSpace collect on LeafLink orders?

Yes. Load the invoice, from QuickBooks or on its own, and we follow up on it, whatever channel the order came from.

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Flat monthly price based on your brand's size, month-to-month, no contract. We go over your price on a call.
