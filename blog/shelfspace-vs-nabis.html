ShelfSpace vs Nabis: sell the invoice, or collect it in your name

Nabis is a licensed distributor that can also pay you early and collect from the store itself. ShelfSpace keeps the invoice yours and gets it paid. Here is the trade, from Nabis's own pages.

By Chris Mitchem · 10/29/2026 · Competitor facts are from each company's public pages as of 10/29/2026.

At a glance

  • Nabis calls itself the #1 licensed cannabis wholesaling platform, distributing brands across California, New York and Nevada.
  • Nabis Capital is invoice factoring: Nabis pays you early, collects from the dispensary itself and charges a fee per invoice.
  • ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link. The customer relationship and the full invoice stay yours.
  • Nabis fits a brand that wants distribution, sales and early cash in one package in those states. ShelfSpace fits a brand that runs its own distribution and wants to be paid in full.

What Nabis is

Nabis describes itself as "the #1 licensed cannabis wholesaling platform," distributing brands across California, New York and Nevada (Nabis). It handles the logistics and wholesale side for brands, and retailers can pay outstanding invoices through its BillPay.

Its financing product, Nabis Capital, is invoice factoring: Nabis buys the invoice and pays the brand early. Nabis says it "is responsible for collecting the payment from the dispensary when due" (Nabis Capital), and its blog says it pays the invoice value to the brand "within 1 business day" (Nabis blog, 05/13/2026).

Nabis publishes example factoring fees of 2.5% to 15% of the invoice, depending on the retailer's credit rating, and says rates can be as low as 1.5%, as of October 2025 (Nabis Capital).

What ShelfSpace is

ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link. The invoice stays yours, the store keeps dealing with your brand, and we never take a cut of what we collect. When a store needs time, we offer 0% payment plans and collect promises to pay instead of selling the invoice.

Where Nabis fits better

If you want someone else to distribute, sell and collect, and you'd rather have cash in a day than wait for the store, Nabis offers all of that in one place in the states it serves. ShelfSpace doesn't move product and doesn't advance money.

Where ShelfSpace fits better

If you run your own distribution, or you'd rather keep the full invoice than pay a factoring fee on it, ShelfSpace collects what stores owe you without taking a cut. Your buyers keep hearing from your brand, not a third party.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nabis Capital?

Invoice factoring from Nabis. Nabis buys a brand's invoice, pays the brand early and collects from the dispensary itself, for a fee that depends on the retailer. Nabis's example fees ran from 2.5% to 15% of the invoice as of October 2025, and it says rates can be as low as 1.5%.

Is factoring better than collecting myself?

It depends on what you need. Factoring trades part of the invoice for cash now and hands the collection to someone else. Collecting yourself keeps the whole invoice and the customer relationship, but you wait for the store to pay.

Does ShelfSpace buy invoices?

No. ShelfSpace collects your invoices for you, in your name, and you are paid when the store pays.

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Flat monthly price based on your brand's size, month-to-month, no contract. We never take a cut of what we collect.
