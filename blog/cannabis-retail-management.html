What is cannabis retail management?

Cannabis retail management is the full job of running a dispensary as a business: ringing sales, tracking inventory in Metrc, staying compliant, and, on the money side, paying vendors, reconciling invoices, recovering credits, and keeping the books straight. Most operators have the front of house handled. Their POS rings sales and tracks inventory, and it does that well.

The other half — the money moving out the back door to thirty-odd vendors — usually runs on email, spreadsheets, and a gut feeling. That's the half ShelfSpace was built to run, and the half this guide is about.

Where dispensaries actually lose money

The front of house is visible. You can watch sales tick up on a screen all day. The money you lose in the back office is invisible until it's already gone:

  • You pay the same invoice twice, because AP runs on email and nobody caught the duplicate.
  • You run a vendor's BOGO and eat the discount yourself — a co-marketing credit you're owed and never filed.
  • You've got a vendor's product frozen on your shelf as cash you can't touch, aging closer to expiration every week.

None of this shows up in your POS. It shows up in your bank balance. Good cannabis retail management is mostly about closing these leaks before they open.
