Expensify Alternatives for Cannabis Companies: What Each Tool Says About Marijuana Businesses

The best Expensify alternative for a cannabis company is one that will actually pay your employees back. Expensify, BILL and Brex each restrict marijuana-related businesses in their own policies, and Ramp restricts cannabis from some features. Here's what each one says, with links, and what to look for instead.

At a glance

  • Expensify lists marijuana-related businesses among the industries it can't process direct payments for.
  • BILL bars plant-touching cannabis businesses from all of its software and payments, and Brex lists marijuana-related activity as prohibited.
  • Ramp restricts cannabis from international reimbursements. Ask it directly about your US entity.
  • Xero is retiring Classic Expenses, so tools that relied on its old expense claims connection need a new path.
  • We built ShelfSpace expenses for cannabis companies: phone capture, approvals, posting to Xero or QuickBooks and paying people back.

Each provider's policy is quoted from its own pages as of 11/05/2026. Policies change, so confirm with the provider before you decide.

Receipt capture is a solved problem. Every tool on this list can read a photo of a receipt. What separates them for a cannabis company is whether they'll take your business at all, and whether they can pay your employees back once a report is approved.

Expensify: receipts yes, cannabis payments no

Expensify's current business bank account article answers the question "Which industries does Expensify not support for direct payments?" and lists marijuana-related businesses alongside crypto-currency and gambling. That's the part that pays employees back by ACH. If you keep Expensify for receipts, you still need a separate way to reimburse people and a way to match those payments to the reports.

BILL and Divvy: closed to plant-touching businesses

BILL's acceptable use policy lists marijuana-related businesses that are plant-touching, with growers, processors, transporters and dispensaries as examples, among businesses that may not use any BILL software or payment products. It also names businesses whose primary customers are plant-touching businesses. That covers BILL's spend and expense tool as well as its payables.

Brex: marijuana-related activity is prohibited

Brex's prohibited activities list names marijuana-related activity. For a licensed grower, processor or dispensary, plan on a different provider.

Ramp: restricted features, so ask first

Ramp's position is narrower in what we could find. Its international reimbursements page lists cannabis-related businesses among industries that can't use the feature, and its Canadian application page says cannabis businesses may be restricted from onboarding. We didn't find a published rule for US cannabis entities, so ask Ramp before you build a process around it.

Xero's own expenses: watch the retirement

If your books are in Xero, its built-in tool is the obvious first look. Xero says employees can snap receipts and claim mileage from their phones. Two things to weigh: Xero's developer changelog says it's retiring Classic Expenses and disabling the old expense claims connection, and it doesn't pay the employee for you, so you still need a way to move the money.

What to look for in an alternative

  • It accepts your business. Read the provider's acceptable use or prohibited activities page before the demo, not after.
  • It handles several entities. Each expense should name its entity, and reports should split by entity.
  • It codes to your books. Each line should carry a Xero account and your tracking categories.
  • It pays people back. Bank transfer, check or a payroll file, not just a record that someone owes money.
  • It follows the IRS rules. Flags for receipts older than your limit and for missing receipts over your threshold, so you stay inside an accountable plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does Expensify work for cannabis companies?

Expensify's help center lists marijuana-related businesses among the industries it can't process direct payments for, which covers ACH reimbursements and its card. You may still be able to scan receipts, but you'd need another way to pay people back. Confirm your own case with Expensify.

Is there a Ramp alternative for cannabis?

Ramp's help center restricts cannabis-related businesses from international reimbursements and says cannabis businesses may be restricted from onboarding in Canada. Ask Ramp about your US entity. If it won't take you, look for a tool built for cannabis that both posts to your books and pays employees.

Does Brex work with cannabis businesses?

Brex's prohibited activities list names marijuana-related activity, so plan on a different provider for a plant-touching business.

Can a dispensary use BILL or Divvy for expenses?

BILL's acceptable use policy, last updated 08/07/2026, says plant-touching marijuana-related businesses such as dispensaries may not use any BILL software or payment products and services.

What's the best Expensify alternative for a cannabis company on Xero?

Pick one that accepts your business, codes each line to your Xero accounts and tracking categories, posts approved reports as bills to the employee, and pays the employee back. ShelfSpace does all four for cannabis companies.
