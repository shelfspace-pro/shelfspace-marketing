ShelfSpace vs Distru: an ERP and collections software, compared

Distru runs your inventory, production and orders. ShelfSpace gets the invoices paid. Here is what each one does, from their own pages, and when you'd pick one, the other or both.

By Chris Mitchem · 10/29/2026 · Competitor facts are from each company's public pages as of 10/29/2026.

At a glance

  • Distru describes itself as a cannabis ERP with a built-in B2B wholesale marketplace, built for cultivators, manufacturers and distributors.
  • ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link.
  • Distru's public pages show invoicing, A/R visibility and a QuickBooks sync. They don't describe past-due reminders, calls or payment plans.
  • Pick Distru to run operations. Pick ShelfSpace to collect. Brands that need both can use both, with QuickBooks in the middle.

What Distru is

In its own words, "Distru is a cannabis ERP with a built-in B2B wholesale marketplace" (Distru). An ERP is the system of record for inventory, production, orders and compliance. Distru's site has separate sections for cultivators, manufacturers and processors, and distributors, and its blog says it "works particularly well for distributors, processors, and manufacturers in Metrc states" (Distru blog).

On the money side, Distru lets you manage open orders and invoice payments, and its financial page offers real-time visibility into A/R by exporting payments or invoices (Distru financial management). Invoices and payments sync with QuickBooks Online; Distru's blog says an invoice created in Distru "shows up in QuickBooks within minutes."

What ShelfSpace is

ShelfSpace is collections software for cannabis brands, run for you: invoices go out in your name, past-due stores get reminder emails on a schedule and an AI phone call once an invoice is badly late, and stores pay from a link. We offer 0% payment plans to stores that need them, collect promises to pay, and send final notice letters when an invoice is badly late. Stores pay by e-check or bank transfer from the pay link, and payments land in QuickBooks.

ShelfSpace is not an ERP. It doesn't track inventory, plan production or file Metrc transfers for your operation.

Where Distru fits better

If your problem is running the operation, Distru is the better fit. A cultivator, processor or distributor that needs one system for inventory, batches, production costs, Metrc compliance and order-to-invoice needs an ERP, and ShelfSpace doesn't do that work.

Where ShelfSpace fits better

If your problem is that stores pay late, an ERP's A/R view tells you who owes you but someone still has to chase them. That is the work ShelfSpace does for you, in your name, every week. If your Distru invoices already sync to QuickBooks Online, ShelfSpace can pick up the open ones from QuickBooks, so the two don't compete.

Frequently asked questions

Is ShelfSpace a Distru alternative?

Only for the getting-paid part. Distru is an ERP that runs inventory, production and orders. ShelfSpace is collections software that sends invoices, follows up on past-due stores and takes payment. Many brands need the first and still have the second problem.

Can I use Distru and ShelfSpace together?

Yes. Distru syncs invoices to QuickBooks Online, and ShelfSpace imports open invoices from QuickBooks, then follows up on them and records payments back.

Does Distru send past-due reminders?

We didn't find past-due reminders, calls or payment plans described on Distru's public pages as of 10/29/2026. Ask Distru directly for the current feature list.

How much does ShelfSpace cost?

Flat monthly price based on your brand's size, month-to-month, no contract. We go over your price on a call.
