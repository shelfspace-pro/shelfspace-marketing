FAQ

Straight answers

I already collect in about 40 days.

Then you're ahead of most cannabis vendors, and the gain is smaller, but it's rarely zero. At $10M in wholesale, every day you take off still frees about $27,000, and the calculator above shows your own number.

I already have an AR person.

Keep them. We take the reminders, payment matching, and Metrc paperwork off their plate so they can spend their time on the accounts that need a real conversation.

Will this annoy my buyers?

Most buyers find it easier: one link, no login, and the option to pay in installments. Every message goes out in your name, reads like your own AR department, and stops the moment they pay.

What about accounts that just won't pay?

Some won't, and we'll tell you so. We work every old balance with reminders, installment offers, and Metrc-matched paperwork, then show you plainly which accounts are recoverable and which are a business decision for you.

Is my QuickBooks data safe?

We only read your open invoices to get started, and nothing is written back to your books without your OK. You connect through Intuit's own sign-in and can disconnect at any time.
